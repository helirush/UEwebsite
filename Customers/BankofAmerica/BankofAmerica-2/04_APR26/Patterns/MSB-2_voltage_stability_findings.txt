MSB-2's voltage health monitoring reveals the pre-commissioning electrical field exhibited severe instability patterns that have been addressed through MPTS deployment. Historical voltage traces showed pronounced triangular voltage-drop formations with frequent sustained dips below the 480V nominal line-to-line equivalent during high-demand intervals. These characteristic triangular wave patterns indicated dangerous device stress conditions where connected equipment faced repeated voltage sag events. The amplitude fluctuations created substantial risk for motor starts, control systems, and process equipment reliability. Post-MPTS commissioning, your voltage profile demonstrates remarkable stabilization with consistent delivery at nominal operating levels. One outage event was detected during the monitoring period, lasting approximately 60 minutes, which appears to be a site-wide power interruption rather than transformer-specific instability.
