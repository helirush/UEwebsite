This page documents a real mixed-load commercial field deployment with three installed MPTS units. The objective is field transformation clarity for owners, engineers, and operators.

Why the AVCO Story Matters in LADWP Territory

At AVCO Center, the primary result is field stabilization: cleaner electrical behavior, lower stress across equipment, and more stable power quality for a mixed-load office environment.

Because this site is served by LADWP as a municipal utility, the financial value of improved electrical performance is not fully translated into the building owner’s bill. Much of the system-level capacity benefit accrues to the grid, while direct bill savings to the customer remain comparatively limited under the current tariff structure.

System Snapshot

  • Class A office tower in the Wilshire spine corridor
  • Approximate height: 14 stories
  • Approximate building size range: 200,000–220,000 sq ft (~210,000 reference)
  • Commercial 480V distribution environment
  • Mixed tenant load profile with continuous operational variability

Field Condition (Before Unity)

  • Load fragmentation across changing tenant and mechanical demand cycles
  • Reactive energy circulation through the distribution network
  • Low field coherence under variable commercial load behavior
  • Hidden capacity loss despite unchanged incoming utility service

Unity Intervention

  • Three deployed MPTS systems in the AVCO distribution network
  • Installed at strategic electrical nodes for high-impact stabilization
  • Design intent: reduce field turbulence, not only improve power factor
  • As-built H240/H490 mix: documented separately with commissioning records

Field Behavior (After Unity)

  • Improved phase alignment during dynamic load periods
  • Reduced reactive oscillation in day-to-day building operation
  • Stabilized voltage-current relationship across mixed occupancy cycles
  • Observed improvement in field stability and power utilization
  • Released usable capacity for operations without increasing incoming supply

Translation Layer

“The system did not increase incoming power. It increased how efficiently the building could use the power already present.”

Primary claim for this site: field stabilization first, with reclaimed usable capacity as a system-level outcome in a real-world multi-tenant office environment.
