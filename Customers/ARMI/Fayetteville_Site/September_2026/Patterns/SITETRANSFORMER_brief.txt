**ENERGY FIELD SUMMARY BRIEF**
**SiteTransformer — ARMI Manufacturing, Fayetteville Facility | September 2026** **Field Condition Summary**
SiteTransformer is carrying an untuned field condition with a baseline power factor of 89.2 percent and monthly consequence costs estimated at roughly $358 per month — approximately $4,357 per year. The 500 kVA transformer is running with its daily supply target at 248 kVA, with 80 to 85 percent of active production blocks breaching that ceiling during weekday operations. No power outages were detected across the 30-day capture window. **Voltage Stability Findings**
The voltage field averages 493.1 volts — 13 volts above the 480-volt nominal — with a swing range from 483.9 to 502 volts across the month. Recurring triangular voltage dips are present throughout September, consistent with shift-start motor inrush events. Device health risk is rated moderate, with chronic overvoltage and periodic demand-sag dips creating compounding insulation and thermal cycling stress. **Reactive and Harmonic Findings**
Reactive burden averages 29.2 kVAR with a peak of 58.1 kVAR. The M view confirms an iTHD maximum of 24.4 percent and a minimum power factor excursion to 62.7 percent — a significant lagging event. Harmonic content is persistently present throughout the month, indicating continuously energized harmonic-generating loads. **Operational Stress Interpretation**
The combination of above-target loading, reactive burden, voltage swing, and harmonic spikes creates a compounding stress environment that is silently accelerating asset wear. Monthly consequence costs of roughly $358 are the financial expression of this accumulated field stress. **Simulated MPTS Outcome**
MPTS correction projects a 99.5 percent power factor target, recovering approximately $285 per month and an estimated $3,472 per year. Reactive burden drops from 58.1 kVAR to 30.3 kVAR maximum. Released capacity of 3.6 kVA is returned to usable headroom. All figures are planning-level estimates. **Recommended Engineering Attention Areas**
Priority actions: reactive compensation scoping for an H240 or H490 unit, harmonic characterization study to investigate the 24.4 percent iTHD peak, monthly voltage trend monitoring, and a transformer thermal baseline inspection. ---
