**ENERGY FIELD SUMMARY BRIEF**
**ARMI Manufacturing — Site Transformer (500 kVA) | August 2026 | Fayetteville Facility** **Field Condition Summary**
The Site Transformer is carrying a compound reactive and harmonic burden across a full production month. Peak demand reached 300.4 kW. Power factor averaged 91.5 percent with a measured minimum of 54.9 percent. Total consequence loading is estimated at $352 per month — approximately $4,144 annualized. The field is functional but chronically stressed during production peaks. **Voltage Stability Findings**
Voltage averaged 492.7 volts against a 480-volt nominal, but a live swing range of 16.4 volts — from 500.6 V maximum to 484.2 V minimum — was recorded. Approximately 20 to 22 triangular voltage dip clusters aligned to shift-start load surges are visible across the month. Device health risk is rated moderate to high. **Reactive and Harmonic Findings**
Reactive burden averaged 29.9 kVAR with a dataset maximum of 56.3 kVAR. A discrepancy between the BT-header MAX of 56.3 kVAR and the M-view MAX of 36 kVAR has been flagged for engineering review. Harmonic distortion averaged 1.6 percent iTHD but peaked at 27.0 percent — coincident with shift-start reactive surges. **Operational Stress Interpretation**
Compound stress events — simultaneous reactive surge, voltage dip, current peak, and harmonic injection — occur at shift starts across approximately 21 to 22 production days per month. The transformer is not overloaded in nameplate terms, but its effective thermal and electromagnetic burden exceeds what active load alone would generate. **Simulated MPTS Outcome**
MPTS correction projects power factor to 99.5 percent held flat. Reactive average reduces from 29.9 to 10.1 kVAR. Reactive MAX reduces from 56.3 to 30.1 kVAR. Voltage dip severity is expected to decrease. Thermal burden on the transformer is reduced. Planning-level sizing: 1 H490 unit (400 kVAR capacity, approximately $200,000 installed estimate — subject to engineering scope and site conditions). **Recommended Engineering Attention Areas**
Priority actions: reactive compensation assignment with CSV-validated sizing, dedicated harmonic survey, voltage event correlation with shift schedule, transformer thermal imaging baseline, and Mercury CSV dataset validation for all numeric anchors. *No outage events detected in this dataset.
