Here's the executive summary on T10. This transformer is running untuned and it's costing the facility an estimated $662,250 per year in combined energy waste, thermal burden, and cooling costs. Power factor averages 89.7% but drops to 81.4% at worst — and the reactive demand peaks at 760.5 kVAR, well above where a compensated field should be operating. Voltage is chronically low — averaging 457.4 volts against a 480-volt nominal — with confirmed sag events in the low 430s during May. That's a 10% sag from nominal, and it's creating measurable device stress on motors, drives, and compressor contactors throughout the facility. MPTS correction projects power factor to 99.5%, recovering roughly 358 kVAR average and dramatically reducing thermal burden. Two H490 units are indicated — planning-level installed cost of approximately $400,000, subject to engineering scope and site conditions. At the current waste rate, the payback case is compelling and the engineering priority is clear. --- Pricing note: these figures are planning-level estimates, not final quotes. For planning, estimated installed investment is roughly $563,000 to $762,000.
