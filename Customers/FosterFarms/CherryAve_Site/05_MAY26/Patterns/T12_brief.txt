**T12 — Foster Farms, Cherry Ave, Fresno CA | May 2026 Field Analysis Brief** **Field Condition:**
T12 is an untuned, heavily stressed transformer operating at 83.9% average power factor with 669.9 kVAR average reactive drag and a peak reactive load of 973.4 kVAR. Peak demand reaches 1,635 kW against a supply ceiling of 1,721 kVA. Annualized energy waste is estimated at approximately $610,000 per year, with a monthly figure of roughly $51,800. **Voltage Stability:**
The voltage field averages 466.0 volts — 14 volts below the 480V nominal reference. Recurring sag events drive the field as low as 442.1 volts, representing a 7.9% departure from nominal. Triangular voltage-drop events cluster across multiple dates throughout May, confirming high device-stress risk for refrigeration compressors, VFDs, and motor insulation systems. **Reactive and Harmonic Profile:**
Peak amperage reaches 2,194 amps. Reactive drag averages 669.9 kVAR and peaks at 973.4 kVAR. Power factor drops as low as 68.6% during stress intervals. Continuous harmonic loading holds at approximately 5.7% iTHD average, peaking at 10.7% — an ever-present thermal stressor on transformer core, neutral conductors, and motor windings. **Operational Stress:**
Thermal burden is measured at 788,678 BTU/hr, requiring approximately 92 kW of active cooling at an estimated cost of about $32.49/hr — roughly $284,570 per year in cooling overhead alone. Eight to nine weekday production cycles regularly reach or exceed the supply ceiling, doing so with a power factor that wastes approximately one dollar in every six delivered. **Simulated MPTS Outcome:**
Post-correction simulation projects power factor recovery to 99.5%. Reactive drag drops from 669.9 kVAR to 94.2 kVAR average. Total annualized savings are estimated at approximately $860,379 per year. Sizing: three H490 units required (973.4 kVAR ÷ 400 = 2.43, rounded up to 3). Planning-level investment estimate: approximately $600,000 installed. Projected simple payback: roughly eight to nine months. **Engineering Attention Areas:**
