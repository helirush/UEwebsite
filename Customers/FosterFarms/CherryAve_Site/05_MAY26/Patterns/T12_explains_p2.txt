T12 at Foster Farms is an untuned transformer under significant and measurable stress. Average power factor is 83.9 percent, with reactive drag averaging 669.9 kVAR and peaking at 973 kVAR. That reactive burden is costing approximately $610,000 per year in waste — and generating enough excess heat to require about 92 kilowatts of active cooling around the clock. The voltage field is consistently below the 480-volt nominal reference, averaging 466 volts and dropping as low as 442 volts during peak cycles. That repeated sag is a direct risk factor for refrigeration compressors, variable frequency drives, and motor insulation throughout the facility. The correction projection is clear: MPTS brings power factor to 99.5 percent, cuts reactive drag from 670 kVAR to under 95 kVAR average, and delivers estimated annualized savings of approximately $860,000. Three H490 units are required for full reactive compensation. The planning-level investment estimate is approximately $600,000 installed — pointing to a projected simple payback of roughly eight to nine months. This transformer is ready for a tuning assignment. --- Pricing note: these figures are planning-level estimates, not final quotes. Final installed pricing depends on engineering scope, labor, and site conditions. For planning, estimated installed investment is roughly $518,000 to $702,000.
