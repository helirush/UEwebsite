**Transformer T15 — Field Condition Summary | Foster Farms, Cherry Ave, Fresno CA | May 2025 | Untuned Field**

T15 is carrying one of the heavier reactive burdens observed in this facility's transformer group. The field is untuned, and the numbers make that unmistakably clear. At an average power factor of 80.8% against a peak demand of 1,420.3 kW, this transformer is pulling significantly more apparent power than the real work being done would require. The gap between real power and apparent power is filled with reactive current — and that reactive current is doing nothing productive while costing money and generating heat every single minute of every operating day.

The reactive load averages 493.1 kVAR and peaks at 708.5 kVAR. That peak reactive ceiling is the critical number for sizing. It tells you this transformer is not dealing with a modest or occasional reactive burden — it is managing sustained, large-magnitude reactive swings driven by the compressor banks, refrigeration motors, and processing equipment that define this facility's load signature. The total wasted energy cost across the year is estimated at approximately $774,142, which includes reactive losses, thermal burden costs, and cooling overhead. That is not a rounding error — that is a structural inefficiency baked into every operating hour.

The thermal burden reading of 542,715 BTU per hour is the physical consequence of this reactive loading. Heat that cannot be recovered or repurposed is simply work that the field is doing against itself. In a food processing and cold storage environment where precision temperature control is a product quality requirement, the HVAC system is fighting both the external heat load and the heat that this transformer's own reactive inefficiency is generating. Until MPTS correction is assigned and tuned, this condition does not stabilize — it drifts. The field will continue to accumulate thermal burden and reactive loss month over month, and the waste cost estimate will continue to climb as operating hours accumulate.
