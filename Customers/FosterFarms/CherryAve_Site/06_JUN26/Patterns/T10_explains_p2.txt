Peak reactive demand hits 776 kilovars. Thermal burden is nearly 492,500 BTU per hour. Total estimated annual waste, including reactive losses, thermal burden, and cooling overhead, is approximately $693,000. The voltage field is showing real stress — four confirmed sag events reaching 434 to 437 volts on a 480-volt system, and one full outage of approximately 60 to 90 minutes in late June. The correction path is two H490 units — 800 kilovars of compensation capacity — at a planning-level installed investment of approximately $200,000. That brings power factor to 99.5 percent, eliminates the reactive circulating current, and recovers the majority of that about $693,000 annual waste. Directional payback is in the range of six to eight months. This field is untuned and drifting. The summer cooling peak in Fresno will compound every one of these trends.
