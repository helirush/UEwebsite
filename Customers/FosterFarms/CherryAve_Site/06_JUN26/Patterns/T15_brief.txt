**T15 Energy Field Summary Brief — Foster Farms Cherry Ave, Fresno CA | June 2026** **Field Condition:**
T15 is a 2,500 kVA untuned transformer operating under compound electromagnetic stress. Power factor averages 81.1%, with a peak demand of 1,366.8 kW and a reactive load peaking at 723.5 kVAR. Total annual waste is estimated at approximately $840,783, including roughly $496,647 in reactive losses and about $36.85 per hour in thermal cooling overhead. No correction is currently in place. **Voltage Stability:**
Average voltage of 478.2V masks a waveform with recurring triangular dips ranging from 408.2V to 437V — representing 9% to 15% below the 480V nominal — distributed across every week of the month. Maximum recorded 509.6V; minimum 9.6V, associated with a confirmed outage event. Two outage events identified, with combined estimated downtime of 80 to 120 minutes. **Reactive and Harmonic Load:**
Average reactive load of 527.8 kVAR, peaking at 723.5 kVAR. Average amperage 953.6A, maximum 1,687.6A. Harmonic iTHD averages 4.9% with a maximum spike of 43.4%. Reactive layer is persistent — present through weekends and overnight — confirming continuous baseline reactive generation from refrigeration and HVAC loads. **Operational Stress:**
T15 is above its 1,371 kVA target line approximately 70–75% of active operating periods. Thermal burden is 587,410 BTU/hr. Cooling load is 104.6 kW. In an untuned field, these conditions drift worse month-over-month. **Simulated MPTS Outcome:**
Two H490 units indicated (723.5 kVAR ÷ 400 = 1.81, rounds to 2). Planning-level investment estimate: roughly $400,000 installed. Projected power factor correction to 99.5%. Reactive loss recovery approximately $496,647 annually. Voltage stability and device stress improve measurably with local reactive support in place. **Engineering Attention Areas:**
