**T10 Air Chiller — Foster Farms, Cherry Ave, Fresno CA | June 2026 | Untuned Field** **Field Condition Summary**
T10 is a 3,000 kVA transformer running in an untuned state, averaging 88.8 percent power factor with a documented floor of 84.32 percent. Peak demand reaches 1,455 kilowatts against a 1,545 kVA supply target. Estimated annual waste totals approximately $693,304 when reactive losses, thermal burden, and cooling overhead are combined. **Voltage Stability Findings**
Average voltage is 456.7 volts on a 480-volt nominal system. Four triangular voltage-drop events are confirmed, clustering in two windows, with sags reaching 434 to 437 volts — roughly 9 to 10 percent below nominal. One full collapse event recorded, minimum 9.1 volts. One confirmed outage of approximately 60 to 90 minutes in the June 21–22 window. **Reactive and Harmonic Findings**
Reactive demand averages 440.8 kVAR and peaks at 776.0 kVAR. Thermal burden is 492,467 BTU per hour. Cooling costs are approximately $36.08 per hour, consuming 102.3 kilowatts. Harmonic distortion averages 5.5 percent iTHD with a 10.4 percent peak — exceeding the IEEE 519 recommended limit of 8 percent at its worst intervals. **Operational Stress Interpretation**
Strong weekday production cadence with Monday ramp-up transients driving voltage-drop clustering. Peak current surges to 1,905 amps against a 987-amp average. Untuned field condition means efficiency drifts downward and thermal burden tends to rise through the summer cooling season. **Simulated MPTS Outcome**
Two H490 units, 800 kVAR combined, restore power factor to 99.5 percent, eliminate the reactive circulating current, and recover the majority of the estimated $693,304 annual waste.
