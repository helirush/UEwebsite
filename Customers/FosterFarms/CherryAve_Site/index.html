Cherry Avenue Facility Overview

The Cherry Avenue facility is one of Foster Farms’ monitored operating sites in California’s Central Valley. Unity Energy has been studying this site since May 2024 to observe electrical loading, field efficiency, thermal burden, and voltage behavior across its four primary 480V transformer fields. Refered to as : T10, T12, T15 and T16

This page serves as the entry point to Cherry Avenue’s measured energy architecture.

Executive Monitoring Snapshot
Monitored Transformer Groups 4 of 4 Primary 480v transformer groups with active high-resolution telemetry.
Coverage Ratio ~80% Measured fields represent approximately eighty percent of Cherry Avenue operating site load.
Total Network Scale 8 fields 4x 480v plus 2x 4160v and 2x 208v electrical energy fields across Cherry Avenue.

Electrical Energy Service Overview

How Cherry Avenue Gets Electrical Energy:

• Shell Energy Supplies the Raw Material

• PG&E provides Infrsturcture for Delivery, Service, Support and Billing

PG&E Shell

Reference Utility Bills

Cherry Avenue receives site delivery through Pacific Gas and Electric Company (PG&E) in California’s Central Valley utility footprint. Supply-side planning and wholesale procurement context for this site includes Shell Energy Solutions as part of the broader resource stack aligned to operational load planning.

Local Utility

PG&E provides regulated retail distribution and grid delivery service at the Cherry Avenue facility.

Supply Portfolio

Delivered power reflects a mixed California portfolio that includes contracted generation, renewables, and market resources.

Regional Grid Source

Power flows through the CAISO-operated transmission and market framework within the Western Interconnection.

Who They Get It From

PG&E manages delivery while Shell Energy Solutions is part of the site’s supply-side procurement context for wholesale generation access.

For Unity analysis context:

Cherry receives bundled grid Electricity through Utility Supply Contracts, not from one dedicated single Generation Plant.
