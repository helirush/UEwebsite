The facility's active power consumption averaged 750.09 kW, while the apparent power demand was 897.32 kVA, resulting in a notable waste of 147.23 kW. This inefficiency translates to an annual financial consequence of approximately $451,414.88.

Voltage health was stable, maintaining a constant 480.0 volts throughout the month, with no deviation observed. However, the facility experienced 139 voltage dips, which could potentially impact equipment performance and reliability. The system generated 352,818 BTUs of heat, necessitating an additional cooling load of 31.03 kW to maintain optimal operating conditions.

Given these metrics, a Base Tune is indicated. The suboptimal power factor and significant energy waste suggest that improvements in energy efficiency could yield substantial cost savings and enhance operational reliability. Addressing the voltage dips and optimizing the cooling system would further contribute to a more efficient and stable power system.
