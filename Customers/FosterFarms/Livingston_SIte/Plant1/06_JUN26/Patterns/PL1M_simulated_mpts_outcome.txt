Transformer: PL1M — Tuned Field Outcome (TBT to TS Optimization Gap)

In the tuned-field optimization narrative, the MPTS Savings view (TS) represents the projected field state when the installed correction assets are performing at their full designed capacity. The distance between the tuned baseline target (TBT) and the TS outcome is the optimization gap — the residual energy waste, reactive burden, thermal load, and cooling overhead that the field hasn't yet fully recaptured, even with correction assets in place.

For PL1M, the TS image data is unavailable, and the Mercury CSV savings projections returned null values. This means I cannot quantify the recovered power factor improvement, the estimated annual savings from reactive loss reduction, the thermal burden relief in BTU per hour, or the cooling cost recovery in dollars per hour. All of these would normally be extracted directly from the TS header values and compared against the TBT header to define the net optimization opportunity.

What I can confirm is the structural intent: when this data becomes available, the TS outcome for PL1M will be analyzed to show exactly how much of the original waste has been captured by the installed assets, what the remaining residual burden looks like in monthly and annual dollar terms, and whether any incremental refinement — such as compensation point adjustment, expanded kVAR coverage, or harmonic filtering — would meaningfully close the remaining gap. These are optimization-level recommendations, not greenfield deployment recommendations, and they respect the investment already made at this site. PL1M's TS outcome section is reserved and will be populated upon data resubmission.
