**PL1-MShop Energy Field Summary Brief | Foster Farms Livingston HQ | June 2026** **1. Field Condition**
PL1-MShop is a 2,500 kVA, 480V untuned transformer operating at 102.3% of nameplate capacity during June peak production windows. Power factor averaged 86.6% with a minimum of 79.73%. Reactive load peaked at 2,265.0 kVAR. Total estimated waste cost: approximately $140,922 per year, with roughly $87,654 attributable to direct energy losses. The field is untuned and trending toward greater stress without correction. **2. Voltage Stability**
Two confirmed sub-nominal voltage dip events in June: 447.2V near June 10th (approximately 6.8% below nominal) and 452.1V near June 28th (approximately 5.8% below nominal). Average voltage of 484.5V runs elevated with compression near the 509V maximum. Device-health risk classified as moderate-to-high for motor insulation and drive electronics. **3. Reactive and Harmonic Burden**
Average reactive load: 194.1 kVAR. Peak reactive: 2,265.0 kVAR. Peak amperage: 952.6 amps. Thermal burden: 184,225 BTU per hour. Cooling cost: approximately $5.32 per hour, 27.0 kW. Harmonic distortion averaged 5.1% iTHD with a peak of 10.1% — moderate, with resonance risk to be assessed pre-installation. **4. Operational Stress**
Approximately 60 to 65% of operating hours at or above target load threshold. Mid-month surge cluster (June 9–14) represented peak stress: near-maximum kVA, near-maximum reactive, and peak amperage all concurrent while the transformer was above nameplate. No outage events detected. **5. Simulated MPTS Outcome**
Sizing: 6 H490 units (2,400 kVAR total). Planning investment: approximately $1,200,000 installed. Projected power factor improvement: 86.6% → 99.5%. Expected outcomes include restored transformer capacity margin, reduced thermal burden, and annual energy waste recovery estimated at roughly $87,654 per year in direct losses, with additional value from deferred capital equipment risk. **6. Recommended Engineering Priorities**
Transformer overload resolution (immediate). Tuning assignment for 6-unit H490 configuration. Voltage dip root-cause analysis. Pre-installation harmonic survey. Monthly thermal burden trending to establish before-and-after measurement baseline. ---
