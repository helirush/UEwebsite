**PL1P2 — Foster Farms Livingston HQ | Structured Energy Field Summary | June 2026** **Field Condition:**
PL1-Proc2 is a 2500 kVA transformer operating in an untuned state with an 86.1% average power factor and a peak reactive demand of 773.4 kVAR. Total annualized waste cost is approximately $261,609 per year. The field is under active electromagnetic stress and decay risk is elevated. **Voltage Stability:**
Repeated voltage dips in the 309–314 volt range occur in clusters throughout every week of the monitoring period. The minimum recorded sag of 309.2 volts represents approximately a 14.5% excursion below 480V nominal — sustained events long enough to stress VFDs, soft starters, and PLC logic. The final week of June shows a worsening trend. **Reactive and Harmonic Burden:**
Average reactive demand is 311.7 kVAR with a peak of 773.4 kVAR — a ratio of 2.4-to-1 confirming high-amplitude intermittent reactive events. Power factor averages 86.1% and drops to 78.57% at minimum. Harmonic contamination averages 5.6% iTHD, compounding both voltage distortion and reactive losses. **Operational Stress:**
The transformer serves a weekday-production-cycle facility with two probable multi-day shutdown intervals observed. Thermal burden is 442,675 BTU per hour. Cooling overhead runs approximately $12.78 per hour, consuming 64.9 kW of additional electrical demand to manage waste heat. **Projected MPTS Outcome:**
Two H490 units (800 kVAR total correction capacity) are the planning-level recommendation. Post-correction power factor projects to 99.5%. Reactive current draw is eliminated, thermal burden declines significantly, and voltage stability improves throughout the motor-switching transient profile. **Engineering Priorities:**
MPTS installation planning, voltage sag root-cause audit, harmonic spectrum study, transformer thermal monitoring, and shutdown event documentation are the five immediate priorities. Planning investment estimate: approximately $400,000 installed. Annualized waste recovery target: approximately $261,609. Estimated payback horizon: 18 to 20 months at sustained production — a planning estimate pending formal engineering validation. ---
