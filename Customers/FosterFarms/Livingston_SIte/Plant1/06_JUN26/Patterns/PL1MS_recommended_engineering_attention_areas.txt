In an untuned field, thermal burden tends to grow as motor loads age toward lower individual power factors. Establishing a monthly thermal burden baseline now creates the before-and-after measurement framework that will quantify MPTS correction value post-installation. This data also informs cooling system sizing adequacy for the current load profile.
