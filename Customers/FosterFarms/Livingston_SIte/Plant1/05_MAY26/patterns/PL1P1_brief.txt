**Energy Field Summary Brief — PL1P1**
**Foster Farms Livingston HQ | May 2026 | Untuned Field** **Field Condition:**
PL1P1 is an untuned transformer carrying a persistent and measurable energy burden. Peak demand reached 316.8 kW against a 238 kVA Daily Target, with power factor averaging 84.7 percent. Annual total waste is estimated at approximately $78,900 per year, including roughly $48,900 in direct energy losses and roughly $26,300 in cooling overhead. **Voltage Stability:**
The voltage field recorded a minimum of 9.7 volts — a near-complete collapse event — during May. The nominal operating voltage is 481.4 volts. Recurring triangular drop patterns throughout the month confirm persistent inductive load switching stress. Device health risk is elevated. **Reactive and Harmonic Stress:**
Reactive average is 99.1 kVAR. Reactive maximum reached 317.3 kVAR. Minimum power factor dipped to 72.52 percent during peak inductive events. Harmonic activity is present but secondary to reactive burden as the primary stress driver. **Operational Stress:**
This field runs near-continuously — no significant weekend shutdowns visible. One confirmed interruption event occurred around May 17th to 18th, with an estimated 45 minutes of downtime. The continuous operating profile means waste accumulates every hour of every day without correction. **Projected MPTS Outcome:**
One H490 unit addresses the 317.3 kVAR MAX. Correction projects power factor to 99.5 percent, recovering the majority of the about $78,900 annual waste. Planning investment is approximately $200,000 installed — subject to final engineering scope and site conditions. **Engineering Priorities:**
Immediate voltage collapse root-cause investigation. MPTS tuning assignment for ongoing waste recovery. Above-target demand and transformer thermal margin review. Load cycling and soft-starter health check on mid-May compressor events. ---
