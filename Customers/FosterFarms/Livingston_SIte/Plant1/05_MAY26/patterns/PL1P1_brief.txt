**ENERGY FIELD SUMMARY BRIEF**
**Transformer: PL1-Proc1 | 2,500 kVA | May 2026 | Untuned Field** **Field Condition:** PL1-Proc1 is running in an untuned state with a measured average power factor of 84.72 percent across 44,640 minutes of May data. The average kVA draw is 185.4 kVA, peaking at 238 kVA at the P95 level. The field is active, productive, and under consistent electromagnetic stress. The BT analysis estimates approximately $4,153 in monthly energy waste and about $78,922 annualized — from reactive losses, thermal burden, and cooling overhead combined. Without a tuning assignment, that figure compounds month over month. **Voltage Stability:** The voltage field spans 462 to 504 volts across May — a 42-volt, 9-percent swing. The average of 481.4 volts is acceptable; the 462-volt floor, repeated on multiple occasions, is not. Recurring triangular drop events indicate reactive inrush and load-switching stress on a 5-to-7-day operational cycle. Equipment connected to this transformer is absorbing sub-nominal voltage stress repeatedly. **Reactive and Harmonic Load:** Average reactive draw is 84.3 kVAR from CSV, with a dataset peak of 317.3 kVAR captured in the BT header. Harmonic distortion averages 5.4 percent iTHD with a 9.7 percent maximum — continuous, not episodic. Power factor minimum reached 72.52 percent. **Operational Stress:** Thermal burden is 103,338 BTU per hour. Cooling infrastructure is absorbing 15.1 kW to manage transformer field heat, at roughly $3.00 per hour — approximately $26,257 per year in cooling cost alone. Three power interruption events totaling an estimated 60 to 90 minutes of downtime were detected in May. **MPTS Correction Outcome:** Post-MPTS simulation projects power factor at 99.5 percent, reactive burden substantially eliminated, thermal burden reduced, and voltage field stabilized. **Engineering Recommendation:** One H490 unit (400 kVAR) sized to the 317.3 kVAR peak. Planning-level installed estimate: approximately $200,000. Engineering scope and site conditions will determine final pricing. ---
