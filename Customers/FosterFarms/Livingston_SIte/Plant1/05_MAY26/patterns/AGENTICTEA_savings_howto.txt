AGENTICTEA — Reactive and MPTS Outcome Notes for Unity Faraday Data availability: No S-image (savings projection) and no CSV reactive data are available for AGENTICTEA. The following notes describe the expected MPTS correction framework once baseline data is in hand. Reactive power concept in plain language: Reactive power — measured in kVAR — is the portion of electrical current that moves back and forth through the system without doing any useful work. It's produced by inductive loads: motors, compressors, transformers themselves, HVAC systems. It occupies conductor and transformer capacity, generates heat, and inflates the apparent power demand your utility sees. An MPTS unit corrects this by injecting compensating reactive current that cancels the inductive reactive load — so the transformer only carries real, productive current. MPTS sizing for AGENTICTEA: Once a kVAR maximum reading is obtained, the sizing formula is: kVAR MAX divided by 400, rounded up. That number is the count of H490 units required. One H490 unit handles up to 400 kVAR of reactive compensation. The planning-level estimate of approximately $200,000 installed is consistent with a single-unit deployment — but this must be confirmed against a measured kVAR maximum before any procurement decision is made. What the S-image will show: When a savings projection is generated for AGENTICTEA, it will display: recovered kVAR, power factor corrected to approximately 99.5 percent, reduced thermal burden in BTUs per hour, and reduced cooling cost overhead in dollars per hour. The difference between the BT baseline figures and the S projection figures represents the total value of MPTS correction for this transformer.
