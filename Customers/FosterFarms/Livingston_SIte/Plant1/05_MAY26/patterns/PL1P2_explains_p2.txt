PL1P2 is a 2,500 kVA transformer running at 84.8% power factor — and that gap from unity is costing approximately $251,000 per year in measurable waste. The reactive peak hit 948 kVAR in May, which is driving nearly 425,000 BTU per hour of thermal burden and 62 kilowatts of active cooling overhead. The voltage field is showing 15 to 18 labeled dip events per month, with excursions down to 306 volts. That is sustained stress on every motor and drive connected to this transformer, every production week. Correcting this field requires three H490 units — sized to the 948 kVAR peak. Planning-level investment is approximately $600,000 installed, with an estimated payback in the range of 28 to 32 months based on current measured waste. This field is functional. But it is not efficient. And without tuning, it trends in one direction — more heat, more waste, more wear — month after month. --- Pricing note: these figures are planning-level estimates, not final quotes. Final installed pricing depends on engineering scope, labor, and site conditions. For planning, estimated installed investment is roughly $510,000 to $690,000.
