Here's the executive read on PL1P1 at Foster Farms Livingston. This transformer ran May at 84.7 percent power factor — meaningful reactive waste, every hour of every day, in a facility that runs near-continuously. The reactive maximum hit 317.3 kVAR. The thermal burden is 103,338 BTU per hour. Total annual waste is approximately $78,900 — that's reactive losses, thermal burden, and cooling overhead combined. There was also a voltage collapse event in May. The recorded minimum was 9.7 volts on a 480-volt system. That's a field interruption that exposed connected equipment to inrush stress on recovery. It requires a root-cause investigation. One H490 unit corrects the reactive maximum. Planning investment is approximately $200,000 installed. The annual waste recovery against that investment is approximately $78,900 per year. Final scope and pricing depend on engineering review and site conditions — but the planning numbers make this a priority for capital consideration. --- Pricing note: these figures are planning-level estimates, not final quotes.
