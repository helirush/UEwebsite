Reactive compensation will absorb much of this post-MPTS, but sequencing improvements add a second layer of protection.

**Fourth: Harmonic Monitoring Continuation.** Peak iTHD of 10.3 percent warrants continued monitoring. If harmonics trend upward as additional variable-frequency drives or electronic loads are added to the facility, a harmonic filter specification may become necessary alongside or integrated with the reactive compensation solution. Establishing a harmonic baseline now protects the engineering scope from underspecification later.
