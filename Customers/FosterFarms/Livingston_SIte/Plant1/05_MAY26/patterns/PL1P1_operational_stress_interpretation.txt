**PL1P1 — Operational Stress and Pattern Interpretation | May 2026**

The production pattern for PL1P1 across May tells the story of a near-continuously operating food processing and cold storage facility. The KVA supply band holds steady in the 150 to 250 kVA range for the vast majority of the month. This is a facility that stays energized around the clock, consistent with cold storage operations that cannot power down without product loss risk.

That continuous operating profile has a direct implication for waste accumulation. When a transformer runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, at a power factor of 84.7 percent, the waste is not a peak-hour phenomenon — it is continuous. Every hour of operation at this power factor is an hour of reactive current flowing through the transformer windings, generating heat, adding to the thermal burden, and incrementally accelerating insulation aging.

In a food processing environment, even a 45-minute interruption carries potential product quality, food safety, and line restart costs that are not captured in the energy waste figures but are real operational consequences.

The target line at 238 kVA is breached upward during the heavier production windows in the month. When demand exceeds the Daily Target, the transformer is operating above its optimized loading point — the operating zone where magnetic efficiency is highest and thermal burden is lowest. Above-target operation means the transformer's magnetic core is working harder, heat generation increases, and the reactive burden compounds. Without correction, these above-target excursions will continue, and the thermal aging trajectory will remain elevated.

The combination of continuous operation, persistent reactive burden, recurring inductive load switching events, a confirmed collapse event, and above-target demand loading makes PL1P1 one of the higher-stress transformers this field would present for engineering review. The field is not in failure — but it is carrying a sustained, compounding burden that will not resolve without intervention.
