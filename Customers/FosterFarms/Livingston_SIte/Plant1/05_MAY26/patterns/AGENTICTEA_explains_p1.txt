Let's talk about what we know — and what we don't — about this transformer called AGENTICTEA. Right now, this transformer is operating in an untuned field condition. That means no reactive power compensation hardware is in place, and — equally important — no measurement baseline has been captured yet. We don't have the demand profile, we don't have the reactive load history, and we don't have the voltage stability record for this unit. Now, I want to be clear about why that matters. An unmonitored transformer in an industrial environment doesn't stay static. Power factor drifts downward over time as inductive loads accumulate — compressors, motors, conveyors, refrigeration systems. Every percentage point of power factor below unity means reactive current is circulating through that transformer without doing any productive work. It generates heat. It ages insulation. It loads the transformer core in ways that shorten service life — quietly, invisibly, until something fails.
