**REND1 — Structured Energy Field Summary Brief**
**Foster Farms Livingston HQ | June 2026 | Untuned Field** **Field Condition**
REND1 is operating in a measurably stressed, untuned state. Average power factor is 79.8 percent, peak demand reached 1,295.2 kilowatts, and the transformer is regularly operating at or above its 1,380 kVA supply target during production hours. Monthly waste is approximately $33,460 — projecting to roughly $662,345 per year uncorrected. **Voltage Stability**
Month-average voltage of 476.6 volts sits below the 480-volt nominal. Seven distinct drop events were annotated, with most dips landing in the 454–461 volt range. A severe sag to 357.5 volts was recorded early in the month, and the absolute minimum of 9.5 volts confirms at least one full electrical collapse event. Device health risk is assessed as high. **Reactive and Harmonic Profile**
Reactive demand averaged 698.6 kVAR and peaked at 1,132.9 kVAR. Peak current reached 1,693.3 amps. Harmonic content averaged 5.8 percent iTHD with an 11.1 percent maximum — a moderate harmonic environment that compounds transformer heating alongside the reactive burden. **Operational Stress**
Thermal burden reached approximately 885,600 BTU per hour. Cooling overhead tied to electrical waste is approximately $25.60 per hour and 129.8 kilowatts. Three planned downtime windows totaling roughly 4–6 days were observed, plus one confirmed unplanned voltage collapse event. **Simulated MPTS Outcome**
Sizing: 1,132.9 kVAR MAX ÷ 400 = 2.83 → **3 H490 units required**. Corrected power factor projects to 99.5 percent. Planning investment estimate: roughly $500,000–about $650,000 installed. Substantial recovery of the approximately $662,345 annual waste is projected. **Recommended Engineering Actions**
Three-H490 reactive compensation array; root-cause investigation of the 357.5-volt sag event; transformer tap review for sub-nominal voltage band correction; harmonic assessment for detuned filter consideration; documentation and review of the voltage collapse event; transformer thermal life baseline assessment. ---
