Accumulated thermal stress from operating in this reactive-heavy condition may have already affected winding insulation condition. Early detection of thermal degradation prevents unplanned failures and informs maintenance scheduling.

**5. Outage Root Cause Review**
The two power interruption events detected in June — estimated at 2 to 3 total hours of downtime — should be reviewed against facility maintenance logs and utility records. Confirming whether these were scheduled, protective relay-initiated, or utility-side events informs both operational planning and any protective relay coordination adjustments needed alongside MPTS installation.
