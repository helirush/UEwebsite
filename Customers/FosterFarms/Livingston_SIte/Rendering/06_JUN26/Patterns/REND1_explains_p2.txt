REND1 is an untuned field carrying a serious reactive burden — and the numbers are hard to ignore. Power factor at 79.8 percent. Reactive peak at 1,132.9 kVAR. Monthly waste roughly $33,460 — about $662,000 per year annualized. Thermal burden near 885,600 BTU per hour, generating approximately $25.60 per hour in cooling overhead. The voltage field is under stress too. A severe sag to 357.5 volts was recorded in June — more than 120 volts below the 480-volt nominal — with consistent sub-nominal dips throughout the month. For a food processing operation, that is a device health risk you cannot ignore. The correction requires three H490 units — 1,200 kVAR total capacity — to bring the field to 99.5 percent power factor. Planning investment estimate is roughly $500,000 to about $650,000 installed. The annual waste this field is currently generating makes that investment conversation worth having now, because this field does not improve on its own. Without correction, heat burden grows, insulation ages faster, and the waste cost compounds month over month. --- Pricing note: these figures are planning-level estimates, not final quotes. Final installed pricing depends on engineering scope, labor, and site conditions. For planning, estimated installed investment is roughly $28,400 to $38,500.
