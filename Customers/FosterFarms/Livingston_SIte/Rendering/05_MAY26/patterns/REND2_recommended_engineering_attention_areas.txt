**REND2 — Recommended Engineering Attention Areas**

Four areas warrant priority engineering attention on REND2 before or concurrent with MPTS commission planning.

**First — Reactive array sizing confirmation.** The four-unit H490 configuration is sized to the CSV-estimated kVAR MAX of 1,324 kVAR. A site engineering review should confirm whether that peak is sustained or transient, and whether staged correction deployment — bringing two units online first and adding the remaining two after load verification — is appropriate for this facility's operational rhythm.

**Second — Motor and drive equipment health assessment.** The recurring voltage dip events documented in the V image — thirteen annotated events ranging from 451 to 461 volts against a 480-volt nominal — represent cumulative insulation stress on every motor and variable-frequency drive connected to this transformer. A targeted insulation resistance and thermal imaging survey of the most load-critical motors is warranted before the next production season, independent of the MPTS decision.

**Third — Harmonic mitigation evaluation.** The peak iTHD of 11.0% documented in the M view is episodic but meaningful. If drive density continues to increase with facility expansion, harmonic mitigation filtering should be evaluated as a complement to reactive correction — particularly for protecting sensitive control and communication systems that may be co-located on the same bus.

**Fourth — Transformer thermal monitoring.** Given the 1,256,097 BTU/hr thermal burden and sustained operation above the kVA daily target for an estimated 65–70% of production hours, a periodic transformer winding temperature survey and cooling system integrity check is recommended as a near-term maintenance action, separate from the longer-term MPTS correction plan.
