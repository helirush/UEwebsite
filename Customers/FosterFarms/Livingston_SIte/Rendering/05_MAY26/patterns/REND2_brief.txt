REND2 is operating with a 73.39% average power factor across 44,640 monitored minutes in May 2026. Average reactive load is 827.4 kVAR. Peak reactive demand is estimated at 1,324 kVAR. The transformer is carrying significant reactive overhead in every production hour — unaddressed and uncompensated. **Voltage Stability**
