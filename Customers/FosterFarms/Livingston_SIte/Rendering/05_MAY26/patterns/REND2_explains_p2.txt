REND2 is running untuned at 73.39% average power factor — carrying 827 kilovars of reactive current on average, peaking at an estimated 1,324 kilovars. That reactive overhead is generating approximately $938,000 in annual waste when energy losses, thermal burden, and cooling costs are all counted together. The voltage picture adds urgency. Thirteen documented voltage dip events across May reached as low as 451 volts — about 6% below the 480-volt nominal. Those dips are clustering at production ramp transitions and stressing motor insulation on every occurrence. Correction requires four H490 units. Planning-level investment is approximately $800,000 installed. Post-correction power factor reaches 99.5%, reactive burden drops to near-zero, and voltage sag events at ramp transitions are substantially damped. This field is not holding steady in its untuned state. The thermal burden is real, the voltage stress is documented, and the cost of inaction compounds month over month. The engineering path forward on REND2 is clear. --- Pricing note: these figures are planning-level estimates, not final quotes.
