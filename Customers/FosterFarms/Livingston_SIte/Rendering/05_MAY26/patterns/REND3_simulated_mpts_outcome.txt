**REND3 — Simulated MPTS Outcome | Projected Post-Correction Field**

The MPTS correction pathway for REND3 is defined by the reactive peak that must be addressed. At 885.4 kVAR MAX, the sizing calculation is straightforward: 885.4 divided by 400 equals 2.21, which rounds up to three H490 units. That is a three-unit installation, providing 1,200 kVAR of local reactive compensation capacity — enough to absorb the full reactive peak with margin remaining for load growth.

Planning-level installed investment for three H490 units runs approximately $600,000, subject to final engineering scope, site layout, and installation conditions. That is the capital entry point. The return side of that equation is the approximately $476,242 in annual waste that MPTS correction is projected to recover. At those figures, the planning-level payback window falls in the range of 15 to 18 months — well under two years for a permanent correction on a transformer of this scale and this level of reactive burden.

What correction delivers operationally: power factor rises from the current 76.9 percent average to a projected 99.5 percent post-MPTS. That means the reactive current that is currently traveling through your transformer windings, your conductors, and back to the utility — all of that circulating magnetizing current — is instead sourced locally from the MPTS capacitive banks. The transformer's effective kVA burden drops. Winding temperatures fall. The thermal burden decreases substantially, reducing both the heat stress on the transformer and the cooling load it imposes on the equipment environment. Voltage stability improves because the reactive source is now local and immediate rather than drawn through the supply impedance — the triangular drop events become less severe, and the voltage rail holds closer to nominal during load cycling events.

The 0.0-volt collapse event requires separate investigation — MPTS correction does not prevent hard outages — but the chronic sub-nominal voltage dips driven by reactive surge loading will diminish materially with local compensation in place.
