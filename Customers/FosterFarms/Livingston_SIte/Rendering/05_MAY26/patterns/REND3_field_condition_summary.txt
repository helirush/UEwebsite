**REND3 — Field Condition Summary | May 2026 | Foster Farms Livingston HQ | 2,500 kVA Transformer**

REND3 is a 2,500 kVA transformer carrying a peak demand of 858.3 kilowatts across a full production month. On the surface, that load level sounds manageable for a transformer of this rating — but the field condition underneath that number tells a different story entirely.

The average power factor across May sits at 76.9 percent. That means for every unit of real work this transformer is delivering to your process, it is also hauling a substantial invisible burden — reactive energy that consumes conductor capacity, heats your transformer windings, and drives up operating costs without producing a single pound of product. The kVAR average is 478.2, and it peaks at 885.4 kVAR MAX. That reactive peak is not an occasional spike — it is a recurring operational reality that the field must absorb every time heavy loads cycle on.

The all-in waste figure extracted from the BT header is approximately $476,242 per year. That number includes energy waste of roughly $293,907 annually, plus thermal burden costs and cooling overhead that together push the total well above the energy line alone. The thermal burden reads 631,000 BTU per hour — a continuous heat load that your cooling infrastructure is working around the clock to counter, at an estimated $18.23 per hour and 92 kilowatts of cooling demand.

This is a field operating under chronic reactive stress, with measurable financial and thermal consequences accumulating every hour of every production day. REND3 has not been corrected, and the data shows it clearly.
