**Priority 1 — MPTS Sizing and Tuning Assignment:** REND3 is carrying 885.4 kVAR peak reactive demand in an untuned state, requiring a planning-level configuration of three H490 units at approximately $600,000 installed. Tuning assignment should be treated as a near-term priority given the confirmed monthly waste rate of approximately $39,687 and the ongoing thermal acceleration of transformer aging.

**Priority 2 — Outage Root Cause Investigation:** Two distinct power interruption events were identified in May, with a combined estimated duration of 150 to 210 minutes. For a continuous-process facility, unplanned outages of this duration carry both direct production loss costs and equipment reconnection stress. The clustering of voltage disturbances and amperage spikes around these events warrants investigation of upstream protective device behavior, contactor health, and any process-side triggers that may be initiating or failing to prevent these interruptions.

**Priority 3 — Sub-Nominal Voltage and Motor Health Assessment:** Recurring triangular voltage dips below the 461-volt lower reference boundary, combined with a confirmed zero-volt collapse event, indicate that motor-driven equipment on REND3's distribution is experiencing repeated voltage stress cycles. A motor insulation health survey — particularly for compressor drives, pump motors, and large HVAC fan motors — is warranted to establish current insulation condition before the next significant failure event. Proactive assessment now is substantially less costly than emergency replacement after a winding failure.

**Priority 4 — kVAR MAX Discrepancy Review:** The cross-check flagged a greater than 10% variance between the CSV-estimated kVAR MAX of 755 kVAR and the image-extracted header value of 885.4 kVAR. Engineering should validate the peak reactive moment with direct interval data to confirm the correct sizing basis for MPTS deployment. The conservative 885.4 kVAR figure is used for current planning purposes.

**Priority 5 — Thermal Burden Monitoring:** At 631,000 BTU per hour, REND3's transformer thermal load warrants ongoing temperature monitoring on windings and oil (if applicable). Establishing a thermal baseline now supports early detection of accelerated aging and provides a pre-MPTS reference for measuring correction impact after tuning is applied.
