Let's talk about what your REND1 transformer is telling us — because this field has a clear story, and the numbers make it easy to read. You're running a 2,500 kVA transformer at Foster Farms Livingston. Right now, that field is operating at 79.8% power factor. What that means in plain language is that roughly 20% of the electrical capacity you're pulling from the utility every day is doing no productive work. It's circulating as reactive current — spinning through your motors and transformer windings — generating heat and cost without producing a single watt of output. The reactive load on this transformer peaks at 1,194 kVAR. Your transformer is carrying a thermal burden of 880,313 BTUs per hour as a direct result. And you're spending approximately $221,000 a year just on cooling costs to manage the heat that a correctable inefficiency is generating. When you add up the reactive energy waste, the thermal losses, and the cooling overhead, you arrive at a total annual waste figure of approximately $658,000 per year on this transformer alone. Monthly, that's roughly $34,400 in documented energy loss. The voltage picture adds another layer.
