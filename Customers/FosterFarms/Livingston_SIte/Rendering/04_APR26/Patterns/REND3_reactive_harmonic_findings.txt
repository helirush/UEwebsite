**REND3 — Reactive and Harmonic Findings | April 2026**

The reactive picture on REND3 is the core driver of the inefficiency story, and the numbers are substantial.

Reactive load averages 480.3 kVAR across the month and peaks at 909.3 kVAR. To put that peak in context: 909 kVAR of reactive demand is roughly equivalent to the apparent power burden of a full additional mid-size industrial load sitting on your transformer — doing nothing productive, just circulating magnetizing current. Every kVAR of reactive demand that flows through your conductors is kVA capacity that cannot be used for real work. At 76.8 percent average power factor, nearly a quarter of your transformer's delivered current is reactive burden.

The M-view amperage trace confirms the consequence: sustained current between roughly 600 and 1,031 amps, with a peak annotation of 1,031.9 amps. That is a heavy conductor and winding current load. High reactive current means high I²R losses — resistive heating in conductors that scales with the square of current. The thermal burden reading of 634,722 BTU per hour is the direct downstream consequence of carrying this reactive mass continuously.

The harmonic picture adds a secondary stress layer. The M-view iTHD reads 5.8 percent average with a maximum of 10.7 percent — more than double the average at peak. The harmonic trace (purple) runs as a persistent baseline presence throughout the month rather than isolated spikes, which tells you harmonic distortion is baked into the operating rhythm of this field, not just an occasional event. The 10.7 percent maximum events create additional conductor heating, can interfere with protective relay timing, and compound the reactive burden effect on total apparent power.

Power factor minimum was recorded at 68.4 percent. At that point, nearly one-third of all delivered current was non-productive reactive flow. For a 2,500 kVA transformer, that represents an enormous waste of installed transformer capacity — and it is happening on a transformer that already routinely approaches its 850 kVA daily target threshold during production hours.
