**REND1 Energy Field Summary Brief — Foster Farms, Livingston HQ | April 2026** **Field Condition:**
REND1 is an untuned field carrying a sustained reactive burden across a full month of food processing production operations. Power factor averages 83.4 percent with a minimum of 70.0 percent. Peak demand reaches 1,348.7 kW against a 1,390 kVA supply target. Without correction, this field will continue drifting toward higher reactive burden and increased thermal stress month over month. **Voltage Stability:**
Twelve sub-nominal voltage dip events are distributed across the full thirty-day period, clustering in the 455 to 461 volt range — up to 5.2 percent below the 480-volt nominal. This is a chronic condition, not an isolated anomaly, and it elevates device stress risk for motors, compressors, and VFDs to a moderate-to-high level. **Reactive and Harmonic Load:**
Reactive demand averages 641.9 kVAR with a peak of 1,113.9 kVAR. Peak current reaches 1,701.1 amps. Harmonic distortion runs at a persistent 5.8 percent iTHD. The thermal burden is 731,399 BTU per hour — approximately 57,500 BTU per hour above the reference baseline. **Operational Stress:**
The transformer is active for roughly 70 to 75 percent of the month at or above the 1,390 kVA target. The reactive tail riding every production shift heats conductors, stresses insulation, and occupies transformer capacity unproductively. **Projected MPTS Outcome:**
Three H490 units sized at 400 kVAR each correct the reactive peak. Power factor projects to 99.5 percent. Total annual waste of approximately $548,238 is recoverable. Planning-level investment estimate: approximately $600,000 installed for three H490 units. **Engineering Priorities:**
Reactive compensation assignment, sub-nominal voltage root cause investigation, peak current thermal profile review, and pre-MPTS harmonic screening. ---
