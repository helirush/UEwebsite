What matters for device health is the production-period pattern: motors and compressors running in the 455 to 461 volt range during load peaks are drawing higher current than they should. In a food processing and cold storage environment, where compressor motors and VFDs run continuously during production, that elevated current draw compounds with the harmonic distortion already present in the field — measured at 5.8 percent iTHD. The combined effect is accelerated insulation aging, elevated contact wear on motor starters and contactors, and increased thermal stress on winding insulation.

Device health risk for REND1 voltage conditions: **moderate to high.** The dips are not catastrophic individually, but their chronic, distributed repetition across a full month of production is the concern. Equipment stress accumulates invisibly until it doesn't.
