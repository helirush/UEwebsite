Your power factor is averaging 83.4 percent across the full month of April. That means for every unit of apparent power this transformer is delivering to your facility, about 16 and a half cents of every dollar is going toward reactive current — current that does no useful work. It doesn't run your compressors. It doesn't drive your process motors. It just circulates, heats conductors, and costs money. Your peak reactive demand reached 1,113.9 kVAR during this period. Your average was 641.9 kVAR. That reactive tail is riding every single production shift — and your transformer is hauling it right alongside your real load of 1,348.7 kilowatts.
