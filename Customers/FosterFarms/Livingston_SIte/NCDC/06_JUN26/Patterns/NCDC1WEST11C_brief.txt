**Foster Farms Livingston HQ | June 2026 | Tuned Field Optimization Mode** **Field Condition:**
NCDC1WEST11C is a high-utilization, near-continuously operating transformer running at 585 kVA supply ceiling with 568.3 kW peak demand and approximately 85 to 90 percent operating utilization across the full thirty-day window. The existing MPTS investment has stabilized the field and elevated power factor to an 85.3 percent average — meaningful progress from an uncompensated baseline. Annual total wasted cost remains at an estimated $207,086, with a monthly waste figure of approximately $10,541. **Voltage Stability:**
Voltage holds in a tight, stable band for most of June — a visible product of MPTS stabilization. Triangular voltage-drop events cluster in mid-June (approximately June 10–15) and appear sporadically in early and late month windows. A 9.7-volt minimum reading is the most significant single data point in the dataset and requires engineering investigation. **Reactive and Harmonic Load:**
The field carries 267.5 kVAR average reactive demand with a 875.9 kVAR maximum peak — the primary remaining optimization gap. Harmonic distortion averages 6.0 percent iTHD with an 11 percent maximum, reflecting active VFD and motor loads that are managed but not fully resolved. **Operational Stress:**

Full optimization to 99.5 percent power factor (TS condition) would recover the majority of the $207,086 annual waste figure and reduce thermal burden by an estimated 55,000 to 95,000 BTU per hour. **Engineering Attention Areas:**
