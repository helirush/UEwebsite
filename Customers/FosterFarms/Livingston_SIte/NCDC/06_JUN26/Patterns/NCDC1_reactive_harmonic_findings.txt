In a 2500 kVA transformer operating environment, sustained harmonic content at this level contributes measurably to transformer core heating, increased eddy current losses, and dielectric stress on winding insulation. It also interacts with the reactive layer — harmonic currents circulate through the system and can partially undermine the reactive compensation that the MPTS equipment is delivering.

The minimum power factor recorded in the TM view is 82.82% — a floor event that coincides with the same mid-month disturbance cluster visible across all four views. The fact that the power factor floor, the voltage drop events, the outage streaks, and the harmonic maximum all cluster in the same mid-month window confirms these are load-driven events originating from the facility's own production patterns — not supply-side anomalies. Large motor starts, refrigeration compressor cycling, or rendering process load switching are the most likely candidates in this operating environment.
