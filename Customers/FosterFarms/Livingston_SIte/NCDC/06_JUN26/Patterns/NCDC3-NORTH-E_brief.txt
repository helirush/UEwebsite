**NCDC3-NORTH-E | Foster Farms, Livingston HQ | June 2026**
**Unity Energy Field Summary Brief — Tuned Field Optimization** **Field Condition Summary**
NCDC3-North-E is a tuned, high-utilization industrial transformer carrying a 655 kVA supply target with near-continuous operations estimated at 90–95% of the month. The existing MPTS investment has delivered measurable stabilization, but a meaningful optimization gap remains: 83.7% average power factor, 372.2 kVAR reactive peak, and approximately $252,475 per year in estimated total waste burden. **Voltage Stability Findings**
Average voltage holds at 479.6 volts, close to the 480-volt nominal. However, five estimated outage events — totaling roughly 40 to 60 minutes — were detected in June, with a recorded minimum of 9.6 volts confirming at least one near-full field collapse. Recurring triangular sub-nominal voltage excursions persist across the full month. **Reactive and Harmonic Findings**
Reactive burden averages 308.2 kVAR and peaks at 372.2 kVAR. Power factor averages 83.7%. Harmonic content averages 5.8% iTHD with a peak of 11.4% — the harmonic trace is continuously active, reflecting persistent non-linear load density from VFDs and refrigeration equipment. **Operational Stress Interpretation**
Thermal burden runs at 333,852 BTU per hour, driving an estimated $84,164 per year in cooling overhead at $9.61 per hour. Peak demand reaches 664.1 kW. The field carries compounded reactive, harmonic, and voltage disturbance stress — all of which have been partially mitigated by existing tuning assets, with a clear residual gap. **Tuned-Field Outcome**
Moving from 83.7% to 99.5% power factor reduces reactive burden by approximately 85–90%, compresses thermal output materially, and stabilizes the voltage rail. Incremental gap closure is the optimization priority, not greenfield deployment. **Recommended Engineering Attention**
Reactive correction gap closure, voltage disturbance root-cause diagnostics, harmonic audit, thermal management verification, and MPTS asset health review are the five prioritized attention areas for this transformer. ---
