**NCDC1 — West11c | Tuned Simulation Outcome | TS View | June 2026**

The TS simulation view for West11c shows what the field looks like when the optimization is fully realized — and the contrast with the TBT baseline is significant. Power factor rises to 99.5%, confirmed by the MPTS MODE target annotation in the chart body. That is up from 85.3% average in the tuned baseline — a 14.2 percentage point improvement that compresses the reactive current circulating in the system and reduces the total apparent power demand the transformer must supply.

The reactive compression story is particularly striking. In the TBT baseline, reactive load averages 267.5 kVAR and peaks at 875.9 kVAR. In the TS optimized simulation, those numbers fall to 40.2 kVAR average and 57.0 kVAR MAX. That is a reduction of approximately 227 kVAR at the average load level and approximately 819 kVAR at peak — an 81% reduction in average reactive burden and a 93% reduction in peak reactive demand. This is not a marginal improvement. This is a fundamental restructuring of how the field is delivering power to the load.

What does that reactive compression mean in practical terms? First, it means the transformer is no longer carrying nearly as much non-working current — the current that generates heat without doing useful work. Second, it means the thermal burden drops substantially, reducing both the stress on transformer insulation and the cooling system overhead the facility must maintain. The TS simulation projects a recovery of 73.1 kVA — which translates directly into reduced apparent power demand and lower thermal loading.

The energy waste and total waste figures from the TBT baseline — approximately $10,541 per dataset in direct energy waste and $207,086 per year in total wasted costs — represent the optimization target. The TS outcome narrows that gap substantially. The remaining residual burden after full optimization is small by comparison, and the direction of the field is clearly toward a tighter, more efficient, more stable operating state. The tuned investment has already captured a meaningful share of this recovery. The optimization work now is about closing the remaining gap systematically.
