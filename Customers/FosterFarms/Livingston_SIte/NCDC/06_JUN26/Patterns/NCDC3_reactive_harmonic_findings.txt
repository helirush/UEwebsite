**Transformer: NCDC3 | Reactive and Harmonic Load | TBT and TM Views | June 2026**

The reactive story on NCDC3 is persistent. Looking at the TBT and TM views together, there is not a single interval across the full June period where reactive demand drops to zero. The green kVAR layer in both views maintains a continuous, non-zero presence — the nature of a facility built around large induction motors driving compressors, conveyors, rendering equipment, and HVAC systems. These motor classes inherently draw reactive current to establish and sustain their magnetic fields, and that demand does not rest when the facility keeps running around the clock.

The average reactive draw is 308.2 kVAR, with the field reaching a peak of 372.2 kVAR maximum as extracted from the TBT header. The TM view independently confirms a Max Reactive callout of 358 kVAR — a difference of approximately 14 kVAR from the TBT peak, which is within normal measurement resolution at 1-minute sampling. Both views are telling the same story: this transformer consistently carries a reactive burden in the 300-to-375 kVAR range, and that burden is not being fully compensated by the existing MPTS deployment.

The harmonic picture compounds this. The purple harmonic trace in the TM view runs as a persistent baseline across the full month, consistent with 5.8% current total harmonic distortion on average and 11.4% maximum. The harmonic content is structured and repeating — not chaotic or random — which is a signature of variable frequency drive topology and switching power supply loads typical of modern food processing environments. The critical observation in the TM view is the correlation: when the Power Factor orange trace dips toward its floor of 80.75%, the harmonic purple band shows slight concurrent elevation. This tells us that non-linear load engagement events — motor ramp-ups, large VFD accelerations — are simultaneously generating reactive demand and harmonic injection. The two stressors are operationally linked, not independent. That linkage is important context for any further optimization targeting on NCDC3.
