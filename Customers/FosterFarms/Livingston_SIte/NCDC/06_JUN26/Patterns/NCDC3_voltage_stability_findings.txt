On a single event, that is manageable. The concern at NCDC3 is repetition. Each individual voltage event is modest. This is the key voltage-health implication for NCDC3: the field is stable, but the concurrent harmonic and sag environment warrants continued monitoring to protect capital equipment longevity across the facility's compressor, conveyor, and refrigeration motor fleet.
