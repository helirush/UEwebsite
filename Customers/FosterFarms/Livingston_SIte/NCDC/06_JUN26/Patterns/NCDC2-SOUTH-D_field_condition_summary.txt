The South-D transformer is operating in a tuned-field state — that's the headline. The discipline of that rhythm is itself a sign that prior tuning work has stabilized the field pattern. What remains is the optimization gap between the current 92.6% power factor and the 99.5% simulation target — and closing that gap is where the next measurable value lives for this site.
