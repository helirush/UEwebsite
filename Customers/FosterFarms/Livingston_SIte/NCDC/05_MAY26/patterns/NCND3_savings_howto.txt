How to read the M P T S savings view: first orient to the harmonized chart, next compare before-and-after field behavior, then focus on reliability and recovery signals before planning rollout. **NCND3 — Reactive and MPTS Outcome Notes for Clerk (Plain Language)** **What the reactive profile means:**
The NCND3 field has a reactive demand (kVAR) that never goes away — it runs every hour of every day because the cold storage and food processing loads never fully shut down. The kVAR average is 296.8 with a peak of 379.4. That continuous reactive burden is what's holding the power factor at 84.6% instead of near unity (close to 100%). Every unit of reactive current flowing through the transformer is generating heat and occupying electrical capacity without producing useful output. **What MPTS correction would do:**
