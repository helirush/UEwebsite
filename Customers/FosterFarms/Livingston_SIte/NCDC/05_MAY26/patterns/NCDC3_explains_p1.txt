And your power factor — the efficiency ratio that tells us how much of the power you're drawing is actually doing useful work — averaged 84.6 percent. That means about 15 cents out of every dollar of electricity pulled through this transformer went to reactive current. Circulating energy. No productive output. The numbers that follow from that are significant. Your reactive demand averaged nearly 297 kVAR and peaked at 379 kVAR. Your thermal burden — the heat your field is generating from that inefficiency — is running at nearly 310,000 BTU per hour.
