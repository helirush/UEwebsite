Here's the headline on NCND3. You have a 2500 kVA transformer at Foster Farms Livingston running continuous food processing and cold storage loads — and it's untuned. Power factor is averaging 84.6 percent with reactive demand peaking at 379.4 kVAR. That reactive drag is generating over 309,000 BTU per hour of thermal burden and costing approximately $234,907 per year in combined waste — energy losses, thermal overhead, and cooling costs. The voltage supply is actually quite clean — 480.1 volts average from Merced Irrigation District — but two confirmed complete power interruptions in May created inrush stress events that compound the chronic reactive burden. The correction solution is one H490 unit — 400 kVAR — at a planning-level installed investment of approximately $200,000. That single unit is sized to bring the power factor to 99.5 percent and target the full annual waste pool. For a facility running seven days a week with no idle hours, the savings accumulate continuously. The payback path on this transformer is compelling. --- Pricing note: these figures are planning-level estimates, not final quotes. Final installed pricing depends on engineering scope, labor, and site conditions. For planning, estimated installed investment is roughly $200,000 to $270,000.
