Here is what the untuned field framing tells us structurally. In an uncorrected industrial field, reactive power (kVAR) is generated continuously by inductive loads — motors, compressors, refrigeration equipment, transformers themselves. This reactive current does no real work. It does not run conveyors, chill product, or power lighting. But it absolutely circulates through your transformer and your conductors, generating I-squared-R losses — resistive heat losses proportional to the square of the current. That heat accumulates in transformer windings, shortens insulation life, and increases cooling costs.
