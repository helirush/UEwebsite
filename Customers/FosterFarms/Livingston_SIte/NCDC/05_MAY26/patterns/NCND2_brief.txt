**NCND2 — Structured Energy Field Summary Brief**
**Foster Farms Livingston HQ | South D — 2,500 kVA | May 2026 | Untuned Field** --- **Field Condition:**
NCND2 is a heavily utilized industrial transformer carrying a consistent reactive burden across the full May operating period. With power factor averaging 93.0% and peak demand reaching 1,001.6 kW, this transformer is doing real work — but doing it less efficiently than it could. The field is untuned, and the cost of that condition is measurable: approximately $127,148 in estimated annual waste from reactive losses, thermal burden, and cooling overhead combined. **Voltage Stability:**
Utility supply from Merced Irrigation District is generally stable, with the voltage band held between 480V and 488V for most of the month. However, the MinVoltage floor of 464V — 3.3% below nominal — is being approached repeatedly throughout May. Three brief outage events totaling roughly 7–11 minutes were identified. Voltage risk is assessed as moderate, with monitoring recommended across subsequent months. **Reactive and Harmonic Profile:**
Average reactive load of 244.9 kVAR with a peak of 351.6 kVAR MAX. Power factor of 93.0% means roughly 7% of all current through this transformer is non-productive reactive current. Harmonic distortion averages 5.7% iTHD with a 10.6% peak — load-correlated and concentrated during peak production cycles. **Operational Stress:**
Thermal burden of 167,322 BTU/hr generates approximately 24.4 kW of waste heat requiring active cooling at an estimated cost of about $4.82 per hour. The facility operates at approximately 70–75% capacity utilization against the 819 kVA supply target, meaning this transformer carries its reactive burden during the majority of productive operating hours. **Simulated MPTS Outcome:**
One H490 unit (400 kVAR) fully covers the 351.6 kVAR peak. Projected correction to approximately 99.5% power factor eliminates the reactive burden, reduces thermal stress, and recovers the majority of the estimated $127,148 annual waste. Planning-level installed investment: approximately $200,000, subject to engineering scope and site conditions. **Recommended Engineering Actions:**
Priority one: schedule H490 MPTS installation engineering walkthrough. Priority two: initiate Mercury full dataset capture for NCND2. Priority three: establish voltage floor trending protocol for months ahead. Priority four: evaluate harmonic survey given 10.6% iTHD peak. ---
