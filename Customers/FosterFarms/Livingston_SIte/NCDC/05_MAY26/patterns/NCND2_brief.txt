**NCDC2 Energy Field Summary Brief — Foster Farms, Livingston HQ | May 2026** **1. Field Condition**
NCDC2 is an untuned 480-volt field serving continuous food processing, cold storage, and rendering operations. The field is functional but carrying measurable reactive burden and periodic interruption events that are generating ongoing waste and equipment stress. Total estimated annual waste: approximately $127,148 per year. **2. Voltage Stability**
Voltage holds near nominal 480 volts for most of the month, with a dataset maximum of 497.8 volts. However, a recorded minimum of 0.0 volts confirms 4 to 5 complete field collapse events across May, totaling an estimated 10 to 20 minutes of outage time. Recurring triangular voltage-drop patterns from inductive load cycling are present throughout all 31 days. Device health classification: high risk for outage events, moderate risk for ongoing cycling stress. **3. Reactive and Harmonic Load**
Average reactive demand is 244.9 kVAR, peaking at 351.6 kVAR. Average power factor is 93.0 percent. Reactive demand pulses dynamically with load cycling, not as a fixed background — confirming the need for dynamic rather than static correction. A persistent harmonic background is present throughout the month. **4. Operational Stress**
Thermal burden runs at 167,322 BTU per hour, driving cooling costs of approximately $4.82 per hour — roughly $42,227 per year in cooling overhead alone. Peak demand reached 1,001.6 kilowatts. In an untuned field, waste and thermal burden trend upward over time without correction intervention. **5. MPTS Correction Outcome**
Sizing: 351.6 kVAR MAX ÷ 400 = 0.879, rounds up to one H490 unit. Planning-level investment estimate: approximately $200,000 installed. Projected recovery: approximately $127,148 per year. Estimated simple payback: roughly 18 to 20 months — a planning estimate subject to engineering scope and site conditions. **6. Engineering Attention Areas**
Priority items: outage root cause investigation, thermal monitoring through summer peak season, harmonic survey prior to MPTS commissioning, and motor/drive condition assessment on largest connected loads. ---
