MPTS correction does not simply recover the reactive losses — it reduces the thermal burden at the source, which in turn reduces the cooling load, compounding the savings across both the energy and the mechanical cooling ledgers.

The kVA supplied drops closer to the actual kW demand — 551 kilowatts — reducing apparent current, reducing transformer temperature rise, and extending the useful service life of both the transformer windings and connected motor equipment. The harmonic environment will also improve as the reactive-driven current amplitude decreases, reducing eddy-current losses in the transformer core.

Without a tuning assignment, this field drifts. Reactive load accumulates, power factor erodes, and the thermal burden grows month over month. Every month that passes at 85.0% power factor is another month of approximately $17,480 in annualized waste that could have been recovered.
