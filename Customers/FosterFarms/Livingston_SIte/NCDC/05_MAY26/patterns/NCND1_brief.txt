Total estimated waste is approximately $209,767 per year, driven by reactive losses, thermal burden of 277,515 BTU per hour, and cooling overhead of approximately $7.96 per hour drawing 40.5 kilowatts. Peak real demand reaches 551 kilowatts against a supply target of 586 kVA. **Voltage Stability:** The field logged a minimum voltage of 9.7 volts — a near-complete field collapse — alongside two identifiable disruption events around May 8th through 9th and May 23rd. Sustained operation above 480-volt nominal and a recorded operational floor near 460 volts indicate moderate-to-high device stress risk for motors and drives. **Reactive Profile:** Average reactive demand of 267.9 kVAR circulates continuously through transformer windings and conductors. Maximum amperage reaches 701.2 amps — approximately 79 percent of rated ceiling. The reactive gap between kVA supplied and kW consumed represents the correctable portion of this field's waste. **Operational Stress:** Continuous 24-hour, 7-day operation with no meaningful weekend drawdown. Two outage or disruption events identified. Cumulative thermal aging and transient stress from voltage disturbances are degrading equipment reliability incrementally. **Simulated MPTS Outcome:** Two H490 units required (445.5 kVAR ÷ 400 = 1.11, rounds to 2). Planning-level installed investment approximately $400,000. Projected field correction to 99.5 percent power factor, eliminating the majority of the about $209,767 annual waste. Estimated payback in the two-to-three-year range at planning level. **Recommended Engineering Attention:** Investigate voltage collapse root cause before installation.
