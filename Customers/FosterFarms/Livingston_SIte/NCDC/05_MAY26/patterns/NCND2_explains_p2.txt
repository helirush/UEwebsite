NCDC2 at Foster Farms Livingston is an untuned field with a clear and measurable problem. Your average power factor is 93 percent, with reactive demand averaging 244.9 kVAR and peaking at 351.6 kVAR. That reactive load is generating 167,322 BTU per hour of thermal burden — costing you roughly $42,000 a year just in cooling overhead, on top of about $78,849 per year in direct energy waste. Total estimated annual waste across all categories is approximately $127,148 per year. The field also recorded 4 to 5 complete power interruption events across May, each reaching zero volts, adding inrush stress to motors and drives during recovery. One H490 MPTS unit — rated 400 kVAR, approximately $200,000 installed at planning-level estimate — is the correct sizing for this field. Projected simple payback is roughly 18 to 20 months, based on the annual waste recovery figure. Final numbers depend on engineering scope and site conditions, but the directional case is strong. This is a field that needs correction assigned before summer heat loads push the thermal burden higher. --- For planning, estimated installed investment is roughly $35,700 to $48,300.
