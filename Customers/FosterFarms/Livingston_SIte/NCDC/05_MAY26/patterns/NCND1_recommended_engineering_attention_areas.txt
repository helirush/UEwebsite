**NCDC1 (West11c) — Recommended Engineering Attention Areas | May 2026**

Several areas on this transformer warrant engineering follow-up beyond the standard MPTS tuning assignment.

**Reactive Compensation Priority:** The 445.5 kVAR MAX reading establishes a clear sizing requirement. Two H490 units provide sufficient reactive correction headroom with reserve capacity. This should be the primary engineering action item.

**Voltage Stability and Motor Health:** The repeated triangular voltage-drop signatures — clustering most densely in the May 2 through May 15 window — indicate sustained inductive load cycling that is producing recurring under-voltage excursions below the 480V nominal. An engineering review of the largest motor loads on this transformer, particularly refrigeration compressors, is recommended to assess insulation condition and VFD parameter tuning. Motor insulation testing (megohmmeter survey) would provide a baseline for trend monitoring.

**Harmonic Content Review:** The 6.0% average and 10.7% peak iTHD readings exceed the IEEE 519 recommended threshold. A harmonic survey to identify the primary contributing loads — likely VFDs and rectifier-front-end equipment — would support decisions about passive harmonic filtering as a complement to MPTS reactive correction. MPTS alone will reduce harmonic-driven heating indirectly by reducing reactive current amplitude, but passive filtering may be warranted if iTHD remains above 5.0% post-MPTS.

**Outage Root Cause Investigation:** Two unplanned interruptions in a single month — particularly the May 8th event estimated at 60 to 90 minutes — warrant review of the upstream supply and switching gear serving this transformer. For a continuous food processing and cold storage operation, supply reliability is a food safety and product integrity issue, not only an energy issue.

**Thermal Monitoring:** Given the 277,515 BTU per hour thermal burden, infrared thermography of the transformer and associated switchgear is recommended on a quarterly basis until MPTS correction is commissioned.
