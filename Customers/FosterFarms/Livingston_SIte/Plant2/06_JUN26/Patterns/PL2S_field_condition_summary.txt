**PL2-1 South — Field Condition Summary | June 2026 | Foster Farms, Livingston HQ | Untuned Field**

PL2-1 South is a 2,500 kVA transformer serving one of the heavier production zones at the Foster Farms Livingston headquarters facility. What the June 2026 data is showing you is a field under genuine and measurable stress — not catastrophic, but trending in the wrong direction month over month without correction.

The daily target for this transformer sits at 1,687 kVA. During active production windows, the field is regularly and consistently pushing above that line — reaching into the 1,750 to 2,250 kVA range — and doing so for roughly 60 to 65 percent of all productive operating hours this month. That means PL2-1 South is operating above its efficiency ceiling for the majority of the time the facility is running. The transformer is not failing. But it is being asked to carry a load that includes a significant and unnecessary reactive burden on top of its real productive work.

The headline waste figure from the BT header is approximately $21,488 in energy waste for the June dataset alone, projecting to roughly $261,446 per year in direct energy losses. When you fold in thermal burden and cooling overhead, the total wasted value climbs to approximately $420,688 per year. That is the annual cost of leaving this field uncorrected.

Power factor is averaging 86.2 percent, with a recorded minimum of essentially zero during shutdown transitions. At 86.2 percent, you are delivering roughly 14 cents of every apparent-power dollar to heat and reactive circulation rather than productive output. The transformer is working harder than it needs to — and the voltage field, the reactive loading, and the thermal data all confirm that consistently.

This is an untuned field. Without a tuning assignment, the efficiency drift tends to worsen across seasons as thermal burden compounds and reactive sources accumulate additional loading over time.
