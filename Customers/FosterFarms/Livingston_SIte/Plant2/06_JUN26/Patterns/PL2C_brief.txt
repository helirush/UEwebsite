**PL2C Energy Field Summary Brief | Foster Farms, Livingston HQ | June 2026 | 2500 kVA Transformer** **Field Condition**
PL2C is running in an untuned state with an average power factor of 86.5 percent. Peak demand reached 526.3 kW against a Max Supply target of 499 kVA. Reactive demand averaged 141.9 kVAR with a peak of 276.8 kVAR. Total estimated annual waste runs to approximately $106,209 per year. Thermal burden is 139,059 BTU per hour, with cooling overhead at 20.4 kilowatts and about $4.03 per hour. **Voltage Stability**
The voltage field shows chronic turbulence throughout June. Recurring triangular dip patterns indicate motor and compressor cycling stress. Maximum voltage reached 502.2 volts; minimum recorded was 9.7 volts — an extreme transient excursion. Device-health risk is rated HIGH. **Reactive and Harmonic Profile**
Reactive demand peaks at 276.8 kVAR MAX. Harmonic distortion averages 5.3 percent iTHD with peaks at 10.6 percent. Maximum recorded current was 599.8 amps. Harmonics run as a continuous base load — no quiet periods detected across the full thirty days. **Operational Stress**
Compounding stress from reactive imbalance, harmonic heating, and voltage turbulence is accelerating wear on motors, drives, and connected controls. Without tuning assignment, reactive demand and thermal burden will trend upward through peak summer months. **Simulated MPTS Outcome**
One H490 unit (400 kVAR capacity) is the sizing recommendation. Power factor corrects to 99.5 percent. Reactive demand drops from 276.8 kVAR MAX to 52.8 kVAR. Projected annual savings: approximately $101,062 per year. Planning-level investment estimate: approximately $200,000 installed, subject to site engineering scope and conditions. **Engineering Priorities**
Immediate MPTS tuning assignment. Voltage excursion root-cause review. Harmonic survey for VFD and rectifier sources. Post-correction thermal baseline re-measurement. Seasonal load projection for July–August peak kVAR growth. ---
