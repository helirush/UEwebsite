**PL2N Energy Field Summary Brief — PL2-3North Transformer | Foster Farms Livingston HQ**
**March 2026 Dataset | Field Mode: Untuned** **Field Condition:**
PL2-3North is a 2500 kVA transformer operating with a persistent, uncompensated reactive burden across all 441 hours of the observation window. Average power factor is 60.7 percent — dropping as low as 49.12 percent — with peak reactive demand reaching 373.0 kVAR and peak real demand at 349.2 kW. Total annualized waste is estimated at approximately $213,478 per year. **Voltage Stability:**
The field operates above nominal for most of the dataset — 484 to 503 volts — with a recorded minimum of 471 volts. Triangular voltage-drop signatures cluster during peak production weeks, consistent with motor-start sag events. Device-health risk is classified as moderate, with compounded stress from simultaneous overvoltage, sag cycling, and harmonic loading. **Reactive and Harmonic Loading:**
Average reactive demand is 164.1 kVAR, peaking at 373.0 kVAR. Average iTHD is 5.2 percent, peaking at 10.7 percent. The combination of deep reactive burden and harmonic distortion creates accelerated wear conditions for VFDs, motors, and transformer insulation systems. **Operational Stress:**
Thermal burden is 277,688 BTU/hr. Cooling cost tied to reactive waste runs approximately $8.16/hr — approximately $71,452 per year. Two operational disruption events totaling an estimated 60 to 90 minutes of combined downtime were detected. **MPTS Correction Outcome:**
One H490 unit (400 kVAR) corrects the full reactive envelope. Projected power factor: 99.5 percent. Direct annual waste recovery: approximately $131,870. Planning-level installed investment: approximately $200,000. Estimated payback: 18 to 24 months. **Engineering Attention:**
Harmonic survey recommended pre-installation. Utility tap review warranted. Outage root-cause investigation advised before scope finalization. ---
