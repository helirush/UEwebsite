**Energy Field Summary Brief — PL2-3North**
*PL2-3North | 2500 kVA | Foster Farms, Livingston HQ | May Dataset | Untuned Field* **Field Condition:**
PL2-3North is operating in a persistently stressed, untuned state. Power factor averages 60.7 percent against a 464 kVA daily supply target, with reactive demand averaging 164.1 kVAR and peaking at 373.0 kVAR. Annualized waste is estimated at approximately $213,478 per year, with a thermal burden of 277,688 BTU per hour. **Voltage Stability:**
Average voltage is elevated at approximately 484 volts above the 480-volt nominal reference, with a confirmed minimum of 0.0 volts — a complete field collapse lasting roughly 60 to 90 minutes. Repeated voltage dips to approximately 471 volts occur throughout the dataset, consistent with heavy inductive load cycling. Device health risk is assessed at moderate-to-high. **Reactive and Harmonic Findings:**
Reactive demand is dominant and continuous. kVAR MAX reaches 373.0, with a minimum power factor floor of 49.12 percent recorded in the active-reactive-harmonic view. iTHD peaks at 10.7 percent — sufficient to contribute additional transformer core heating and drive-electronics stress. **Operational Stress:**
In an untuned field, reactive burden, thermal losses, voltage instability, and harmonic content compound each other. Without tuning assignment, efficiency drifts lower and heat burden tends to rise month over month. One confirmed outage event adds a reliability dimension beyond energy cost. **MPTS Simulation Outcome:**
MPTS correction projects power factor to 99.5 percent, collapsing average reactive demand from 164.1 to approximately 9.6 kVAR and MAX demand from 373.0 to approximately 35.1 kVAR. Projected annualized savings: approximately $207,792 per year. **Engineering Recommendation:**
One H490 unit (400 kVAR) covers peak reactive demand. Planning-level installed investment: approximately $200,000, subject to engineering scope and site conditions. Planning-level payback: approximately 11 to 12 months. Priority engineering items include outage root-cause investigation, voltage tap review, harmonic audit, and cooling system capacity assessment. ---
