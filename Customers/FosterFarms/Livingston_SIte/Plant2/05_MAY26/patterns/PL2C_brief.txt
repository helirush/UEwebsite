PL2-2Center is operating with an average power factor of 83.9 percent against a daily target of 434 kVA. Peak demand reaches 551.3 kW. The reactive maximum of 311 kVAR represents a persistent, uncompensated inductive burden accumulating heat and waste every operating hour. Without tuning, field efficiency continues to drift downward month over month. **Voltage Stability**
