**PL2-1South Energy Field Summary Brief**
**Foster Farms, Livingston HQ | 2,500 kVA Transformer | March 13–31, 2026 | 442 Hours** --- **Field Condition**
PL2-1South is operating in a persistently stressed, untuned state. Power factor is averaging 85.7%, with a peak reactive demand of 927.9 kVAR against a 1,584 kVA supply target. Total estimated annual waste is approximately $424,031, combining reactive losses, thermal burden, and cooling overhead. This transformer is carrying a chronic efficiency deficit on every production shift. **Voltage Stability**
Voltage averages 481.4 volts with a confirmed minimum of 0.0 volts — a complete collapse event. Recurring triangular voltage dips cluster during peak production windows and sustain below the 480-volt nominal reference. Device-health risk is rated moderate to high, with motor and drive equipment under elevated thermal and mechanical stress on every cycle. **Reactive and Harmonic Loading**
Average reactive load is 562.5 kVAR. Peak reactive demand reaches 927.9 kVAR. iTHD runs at 15.6%, nearly double the IEEE 519 upper threshold. These two stressors combine to produce a thermal burden of 556,819 BTU per hour and cooling costs of approximately $16.13 per hour — a compounding cost that runs continuously through every production shift. **Operational Stress**
Approximately 60% of active production hours push above the supply target. Two outage events totaling roughly 20 to 30 minutes of downtime were confirmed in the 19-day window. In a food processing environment, every unplanned interruption carries food safety, restart, and production continuity costs beyond the energy waste figure. **Projected MPTS Outcome**
Three H490 units are required, delivering 1,200 kVAR of correction capacity. Projected power factor improvement moves from 85.7% to approximately 99.5%. Reactive burden is eliminated at the source, thermal burden drops substantially, voltage stability improves, and the majority of the approximately $424,031 annual waste is recoverable. Planning-level installed investment is approximately $600,000, subject to engineering scope and site conditions. **Engineering Attention Areas**
Priority items include outage root cause investigation, a dedicated harmonic survey, transformer thermographic inspection, neutral conductor adequacy review, and a load scheduling assessment to flatten reactive demand peaks during shift startup. ---
