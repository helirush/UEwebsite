The 373.0 kVAR peak and 164.1 kVAR average reactive demand on PL2-3North represent magnetic energy that the system borrows from the supply on every AC cycle and returns without doing useful work. This back-and-forth reactive current flow is invisible to production output but fully visible to thermal losses. Every amp of reactive current heats conductors, transformer cores, and switchgear contacts identically to a real working amp — it just produces heat instead of output. **MPTS sizing for PL2-3North:**
