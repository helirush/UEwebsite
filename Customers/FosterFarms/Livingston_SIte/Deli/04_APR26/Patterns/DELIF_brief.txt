**DELIF — Deli F Transformer | Energy Field Brief | April 2026** **Field Condition:**
The Deli F transformer is running an untuned field at 85.3 percent power factor — meaning roughly 15 percent of every kilovolt-amp delivered is wasted as reactive circulation. Annual waste is estimated at approximately $276,493. Monthly cost during April was roughly $14,056. Thermal burden is 361,073 BTU per hour. Peak demand reached 1,668.3 kilowatts against a 1,591 kVA supply target. **Voltage Stability:**
Average voltage is 480.0 volts, but the field recorded dips to 458.2 volts — 4.5 percent below nominal — during production load transitions. Triangular dip events cluster near April 14 and April 26–27. One confirmed outage event, estimated at 40 to 60 minutes, was recorded near April 14. Device health risk is rated moderate to high. **Reactive and Harmonic Profile:**
Average reactive draw is 407.4 kVAR, peaking at 726.7 kVAR. Harmonic distortion averages 5.4 percent iTHD with a maximum of 10.5 percent — double the IEEE 519 recommended limit at peak. The green reactive layer is visibly thick under every production block in the baseline view. **Operational Stress:**
18 to 20 weekday production blocks visible across April. The majority press at or above the 1,591 kVA target. Load transitions produce amperage spikes to 3,221.7 amps against an average of 809.1 amps — a 4:1 peak-to-average ratio that drives both the voltage dips and harmonic spikes. **Projected MPTS Outcome:**
Two H490 units — 800 kVAR combined — bring power factor to 99.5 percent, neutralize the reactive waste stream, reduce thermal burden, and stabilize voltage during load transitions. Planning-level investment: approximately $200,000 installed. Final pricing subject to engineering scope and site conditions. **Engineering Priorities:**
Reactive compensation first. Voltage monitoring post-installation. Formal harmonic survey recommended. Outage root cause review advised. ---
