The Active-Reactive-Harmonic view confirms what the numbers suggest: the green kVAR trace is a constant companion to the red amperage trace — it never fully collapses, even during off-peak valleys. The minimum power factor dipping to 69.81% during specific load transitions is a critical finding. Harmonics, while not the dominant stressor here, are a continuous background condition. The 5.1% average iTHD with a 10.2% maximum indicates that harmonic content approximately doubles during load-switching or drive-intensive moments. This is consistent with variable-frequency drive operation, compressor staging, and conveyor load changes typical of food processing circuits. Harmonic content at these levels adds supplementary thermal burden to transformer windings and can interact with power factor correction equipment if not properly filtered.
