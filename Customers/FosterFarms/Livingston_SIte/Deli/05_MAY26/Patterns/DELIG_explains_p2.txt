The Deli.G transformer spent May operating at over 100 percent of its nameplate capacity — 2,494.3 kVA against a 2,500 kVA rating. That's the headline. The root cause is an 86 percent power factor in an untuned field. Reactive demand peaked at 1,779.6 kVAR, driving thermal burden to 280,066 BTU per hour and total annual waste to approximately $215,300 per year. Voltage dips to 460 volts during peak loading events, which is a 4 percent sag below nominal — a recurring stress on every piece of sensitive equipment downstream. MPTS correction for this field calls for 5 H490 units, at a planning-level installed estimate of approximately $1,000,000. Post-correction, the field projects to 99.5 percent power factor — relieving transformer overloading, stabilizing voltage, and recovering the full annual waste stream. This is a transformer that is working at its limit and needs engineering attention soon. --- Pricing note: these figures are planning-level estimates, not final quotes. Final installed pricing depends on engineering scope, labor, and site conditions. For planning, estimated installed investment is roughly $183,000 to $248,000.
