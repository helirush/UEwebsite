**DELIG — Deli.G Transformer | Structured Energy Field Summary Brief | May 2026** **1. Field Condition:**
The Deli.G 2,500 kVA transformer is operating in an untuned field state with a power factor of 86.0 percent and a peak loading of 102.3 percent of nameplate capacity — 2,494.3 kVA against a 2,500 kVA rating. The field is generating approximately $215,326 per year in total waste cost across reactive losses, thermal burden, and cooling overhead. **2. Voltage Stability:**
Average voltage holds at 480.4V with recurring operational dips to approximately 460V during peak load events — roughly 4 percent below the 480V nominal. Fine-grain oscillation is visible throughout the voltage trace, consistent with harmonic injection. Two outage events are observed near the end of May. **3. Reactive and Harmonic Loading:**
Average reactive load is 319.8 kVAR with a peak of 1,779.6 kVAR. Harmonic distortion averages 5.1 percent iTHD with a peak of 10.6 percent — levels consistent with VFD-driven industrial loads creating a combined reactive-harmonic stress environment. **4. Operational Stress:**
Peak amperage reached 2,864.9 amps. Thermal burden is 280,066 BTU per hour with cooling costs of about $8.21 per hour — about $71,933 per year. The transformer is running at its physical ceiling during production peaks on a recurring five-day weekly cycle. **5. MPTS Correction Projection:**
