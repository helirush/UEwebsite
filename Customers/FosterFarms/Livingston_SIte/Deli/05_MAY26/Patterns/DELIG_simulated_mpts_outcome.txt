**Transformer: Deli.G — DELIG | Projected MPTS Correction Impact**

The S view provides the projected post-MPTS field state for Deli.G, and the contrast with the baseline is significant.

Beginning with sizing: applying the standard MPTS sizing rule to Deli.G's kVAR MAX of 1,779.6 kVAR — divide by 400, round up — yields a requirement of 4.45, rounded up to **5 H490 units**. At the planning-level estimate of approximately $200,000 installed per H490 unit, the planning-range investment for full correction on Deli.G would be approximately **$1,000,000 installed**, with final pricing dependent on engineering scope, site conditions, and configuration. That is a planning-level estimate, not a binding quote.

Post-MPTS, the S view projects power factor correction to 99.5 percent — up from the current 86.0 percent baseline. That 13.5 percentage point improvement in power factor directly reduces the reactive current circulating in the transformer, which reduces apparent power demand, reduces thermal burden, and relieves the transformer loading that currently pushes it to 102.3 percent of nameplate capacity. With reactive compensation in place, the same real-work load would be delivered at a substantially lower apparent power draw — creating headroom on the transformer that does not currently exist.

The projected annual waste recovery approaches the full $215,326/yr total wasted figure — with the reactive loss recovery component of approximately $133,139/yr and the thermal and cooling component of approximately $71,933/yr being the primary recovery drivers. The carbon recovery of 266.3 MT/yr CO2e equivalent is a secondary benefit worth noting for sustainability reporting purposes.

Voltage stability also benefits directly: reactive compensation reduces the reactive current spikes that cause the 460V voltage dips observed during peak loading. A corrected field would deliver flatter, more stable voltage to motor controls, VFDs, and refrigeration equipment — reducing the device-stress cycle that currently repeats every production shift.
