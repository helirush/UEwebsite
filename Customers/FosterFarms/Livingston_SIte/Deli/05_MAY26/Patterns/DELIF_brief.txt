**DELI F — ENERGY FIELD SUMMARY BRIEF | May 2026 | Untuned Field** **1. Field Condition**
Deli F is a 2,500 kVA transformer running an untuned field with an 85.3% average power factor. Peak demand reaches 1,739.8 kW. Reactive average is 361.9 kVAR with a peak of 1,150.1 kVAR. Annual all-in waste is approximately $248,128 — including approximately $153,543 in direct energy waste and approximately $82,759 in cooling overhead. **2. Voltage Stability**
Average voltage holds at 480.6 volts, but a confirmed 0.0-volt collapse event occurred in May. A documented sag to 456.9 volts — 4.8% below nominal — recurs in production-correlated clusters. Voltage swings span nearly 40 volts across the month. **3. Reactive and Harmonic Profile**
kVAR MAX of 1,150.1 drives the sizing requirement. Current spikes reach 1,865.4 amps — nearly double the 928-amp monthly average. Worst-case power factor hits 72.58%. Harmonic distortion averages 5.2% iTHD, peaking at 10.4% — secondary concern to reactive magnitude. **4. Operational Stress**
Thermal burden is 323,371 BTU/hr. Every 22-day production cycle applies recurring voltage stress, reactive surge, and current spike loading to this transformer. The untuned field is generating cumulative mechanical and thermal damage across every shift. **5. MPTS Correction Outcome**
Post-MPTS simulation shows power factor lifting to 99.5% with full uniformity. Reactive average drops from 361.9 to 64.1 kVAR. Sizing: 3 H490 units required (1,150.1 ÷ 400 = 2.88, rounded up to 3). Planning-level investment approximately $600,000 installed. Estimated payback approximately 28 to 30 months. **6. Engineering Priorities**
Investigate the 0.0V collapse event. Confirm kVAR MAX peak origin. Thermal-scan primary connections. Evaluate load sequencing. Verify transformer thermal rating against 323,371 BTU/hr burden. ---
