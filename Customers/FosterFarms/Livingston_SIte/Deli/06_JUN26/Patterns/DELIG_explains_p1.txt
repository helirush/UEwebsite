On an industrial transformer feeding food processing and cold storage loads, that means roughly 14 percent of every amp your transformer is pulling from the grid is doing no productive work. It's magnetizing current — reactive drag — and it's riding on your conductors, heating your transformer core, and destabilizing your voltage field every hour of every shift. The reactive demand tells you exactly how large that problem is. Your average reactive load is 328 kVAR. But at peak moments — compressor startups, line inrush, HVAC staging — that spikes to 1,434 kVAR MAX. That's a fourfold swing from average to peak, happening repeatedly in a field that has no correction mechanism in place. Your voltage field is reflecting that stress directly.
