Here's the executive summary for FSO-3 at Foster Farms Livingston. The MPTS assets already deployed on this 2,500 kVA transformer have done meaningful work — power factor is at 91.5 percent, and that's a real improvement over an uncorrected field. But the job isn't finished. Reactive demand still averages 202 kVAR and peaks at 334 kVAR. Thermal burden is running at nearly 150,000 BTU per hour. And total annual waste — energy, heat, and cooling combined — sits at approximately $115,776. The voltage field shows a 460-volt minimum against a 480-volt nominal, two confirmed power interruption events in June, and continuous triangular-dip signatures from motor loads throughout the month. In a cold storage and food processing environment, voltage instability and unplanned outages carry operational and food safety consequences that compound the pure energy cost picture. The TS simulation confirms the target state: 99.5 percent power factor, reactive demand dropping to approximately 45.6 kVAR, reduced thermal burden, and a measurably more stable voltage floor. The engineering path to get there runs through reactive tuning of the existing MPTS deployment, a root cause investigation on the June 13th event, and a harmonic assessment at VFD load nodes. The investment foundation is solid. The optimization opportunity is clear and well-defined. ---
