**FSO-3 — Foster Farms, Livingston HQ | Field Condition Summary | June 2026**

FSO-3 is a 2,500 kVA transformer serving one of Foster Farms' most operationally demanding facilities. The June 2026 field tells a story of meaningful progress already delivered — but with clear optimization headroom still on the table.

The tuned baseline power factor sits at 91.5 percent, which represents a genuinely improved field compared to an uncorrected industrial site of this scale and load character. The MPTS assets already deployed have moved this transformer away from the deep reactive waste territory you'd expect from a food processing and cold storage operation running heavy motor loads. That's a real, measurable win.

But 91.5 percent power factor is not a finished field. The remaining reactive burden — averaging 202.4 kVAR and peaking at 334.2 kVAR — is still generating $70,580 in annual energy waste and $39,830 in annual cooling costs against the transformer's thermal envelope. Total wasted energy cost runs to approximately $115,776 per year when all burden components are combined. The thermal burden rate is running at 149,513 BTU per hour. On a 2,500 kVA transformer that's already been invested in, that remaining gap is where the next round of optimization value lives.

The field is also not voltage-quiet. Voltage is active and oscillatory across the full thirty-day period, with a confirmed low of 460 volts line-to-line — 4.2 percent below nominal — and two identified power interruption events during the month. The load character is reactive-heavy and dynamic, driven by the motor-intensive nature of cold storage compression, processing line drives, and HVAC systems. Harmonic distortion averages 5.6 percent with peaks reaching 10.0 percent, adding transformer winding heat that compounds the thermal burden picture.

The optimization gap between the current tuned state and the full MPTS target outcome is well-defined and quantifiable. June 2026 is a month that demonstrates both what the existing investment has already achieved and exactly where additional tuning effort will continue to return value.
