**FSO Transformer — Field Condition Summary | Foster Farms Livingston HQ | June 2026**

The FSO transformer is operating in a tuned-field state — MPTS assets have been deployed and the simulation view confirms substantial reactive compensation is achievable and in progress. That said, the current measured field in June still carries meaningful residual burden. The baseline tuned-target view (TBT) shows an 86.1% average power factor against a 757 kVA supply ceiling, with a peak demand of 763.3 kilowatts. That power factor reading tells you the field is running with significant reactive drag still present — roughly 250 kVAR of average reactive load pulling on your distribution infrastructure every hour of every day this month.

The total wasted cost picture from the TBT header is substantial: approximately $207,459 per year in combined energy waste and thermal overhead. Monthly waste alone runs approximately $9,993. The thermal burden sits at 277,397 BTU per hour — that's a heavy heat load on your transformer and the spaces around it. Cooling costs are running at approximately $8.75 per hour, consuming 42.6 kilowatts just to manage heat that the reactive field is generating.

The field is not in distress — voltage is well-centered, the supply trace is vigorous and largely above target, and the tuned-field infrastructure is clearly doing work. But the optimization gap between where this field is today and where the TS simulation shows it can go is real and quantified. The TS outcome projects 99.5% power factor, dropping average reactive burden from 250.5 kVAR down to 36.5 kVAR. That's the remaining optimization target for this transformer.

Two brief outage events were detected in the voltage and BT layers — approximately 4 to 6 minutes of combined interruption around June 7th and 9th. These are short, but they warrant logging and review as part of ongoing field stewardship.
