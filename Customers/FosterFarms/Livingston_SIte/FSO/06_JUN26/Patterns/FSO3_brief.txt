**ENERGY FIELD SUMMARY BRIEF — FSO-3 | Foster Farms, Livingston HQ**
**June 2026 | 2,500 kVA Transformer | Tuned-Field Optimization Mode** **Field Condition:**
FSO-3 is a tuned field with deployed MPTS assets that have delivered meaningful reactive correction. Power factor stands at 91.5 percent — a real improvement over an uncorrected baseline — but the field is not yet at its target state. Annual total waste remains at approximately $115,776, with energy waste at $70,580 per year, and cooling costs at $39,830 per year. Thermal burden runs at 149,513 BTU per hour. **Voltage Stability:**
The voltage field is active and oscillatory across all thirty days. The confirmed minimum is 460 volts — 4.2 percent below nominal — with a peak of 498 volts. A severe mid-month disturbance at June 13th represents the most significant voltage event. Triangular motor-start dip signatures are distributed continuously across the month, indicating persistent reactive-driven voltage stress. **Reactive and Harmonic Loading:**
Reactive demand averages 202.4 kVAR and peaks at 334.2 kVAR. Harmonic distortion averages 5.6 percent with a maximum of 10.0 percent. Minimum power factor drops to 81.51 percent during load transitions. These values confirm structural reactive and harmonic burden that the existing MPTS tuning has partially addressed. **Operational Stress:**
Two power interruption events detected — approximately June 13th and June 28th — with estimated total downtime of 90 to 120 minutes. In a food processing and cold storage environment, these events carry cold chain, compliance, and production reset implications well beyond the raw minutes of interruption. **MPTS Optimization Outcome (TS Target):**
Full tuning to 99.5 percent power factor reduces reactive demand from 202.4 kVAR to approximately 45.6 kVAR average, compresses thermal burden, reduces cooling overhead, and raises the voltage floor to reduce VFD trip risk. The $115,776 annual total waste represents the full recovery ceiling for this transformer. **Recommended Engineering Actions:**
Root cause investigation for the June 13th event; MPTS compensation tuning review targeting the 334.2 kVAR reactive peak; harmonic audit at VFD load nodes; VFD undervoltage trip tolerance review; and secondary interruption follow-up for the June 28th event. ---
