The reactive burden on BNR-1 is structural, not episodic. Variable-frequency drives, soft starters, and rectifier-based loads cycling through production states are the most likely sources. At a food processing facility of this scale, these loads are unavoidable, but their harmonic contribution is continuously aging the transformer insulation, adding to core losses, and contributing to the thermal burden even during periods when the kVA loading itself looks moderate.

The amperage trace in the M view ran a persistent band with a peak annotation of 1,930.6 amps, and the BT header confirms a max amperage of 2,067.7 amps — telling you this transformer is seeing full-load current conditions regularly throughout the production cycle. The combined effect of reactive drag, harmonic distortion, and high-current loading is a thermally stressed transformer operating without correction.
