BNR-2 is a lightly loaded transformer — averaging 16.5 kVA against a 17.6 kVA p95 target — operating in an untuned field state for the full 31-day May dataset. Power factor averages 96.47 percent. The field is stable in structure but carrying reactive, thermal, and harmonic burden that grows without correction. **Voltage Stability:**
Average voltage is 279.5 V line-to-neutral, consistent with nominal. Dips below 480-volt nominal are confirmed and documented by the system's own MinVoltage reference line at 467 volts. **Reactive and Harmonic:**

Total estimated annual waste is approximately $2,917, including energy losses, thermal burden, and cooling overhead.
