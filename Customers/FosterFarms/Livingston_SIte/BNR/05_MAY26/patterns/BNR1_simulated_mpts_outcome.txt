What does that correction buy operationally? The 2,067.7-amp peak current draw decreases as apparent power demand falls with improved power factor, reducing I²R losses in the conductors and transformer windings proportionally.

The BT header estimated total waste of approximately $425,113 per year. The S view represents the majority of that waste being recovered annually.
