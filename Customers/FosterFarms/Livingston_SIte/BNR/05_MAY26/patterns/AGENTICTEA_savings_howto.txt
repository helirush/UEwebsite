Here is the framework Clerk should use when speaking with the facility contact about correction opportunity: Reactive power, measured in kVAR, is the portion of electrical demand that does not perform useful work but still loads the transformer and shows up on the utility bill. In an untuned field like AGENTICTEA, reactive demand is present and uncompensated. MPTS correction — specifically the H490 unit — is designed to compensate that reactive load at the source, pushing power factor from wherever it currently sits up to approximately 99.5 percent. The practical results of that correction: apparent demand drops, utility capacity charges decrease, transformer thermal burden is reduced in BTU per hour, and cooling energy requirements fall. All of those reductions have a dollar value that accumulates month over month. The planning-level installed estimate for an H490 unit is approximately $200,000. That figure is a planning estimate — not a binding quote — and final pricing depends on engineering scope, conduit routing, panel access, and site conditions unique to this facility.
