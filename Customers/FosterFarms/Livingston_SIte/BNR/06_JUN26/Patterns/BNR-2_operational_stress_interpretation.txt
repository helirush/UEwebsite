That differential — roughly 155 BTU per hour above baseline — represents the residual heat load attributable to the remaining optimization gap. On a continuous basis across a full year, that thermal overhead accumulates. The power factor performance, while improved, shows a minimum dip to 85.3% in the TBT record. That minimum floor — occurring at some point during the month, likely during low-load or post-outage conditions — represents a transient excursion below the 95.76% MIN reference established in the TM view. These momentary low-power-factor events carry disproportionate thermal and reactive stress relative to their duration, and they are the primary remaining vulnerability in an otherwise well-managed field.
