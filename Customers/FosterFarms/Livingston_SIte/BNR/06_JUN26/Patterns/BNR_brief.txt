BNR enters June 2026 as an invested, partially tuned field carrying a meaningful optimization gap. Annual waste estimates approximately $274,706 in reactive losses, with total wasted burden reaching approximately $449,367 per year including thermal and cooling overhead. **Voltage Stability Findings**

Four to five outage events totaling an estimated 5 to 10 minutes were detected. The voltage profile creates cumulative stress for motors, drives, and process controls in this cold-chain environment. **Reactive and Harmonic Findings**

Layered reactive, voltage, and harmonic stress is compounding thermal aging of transformer insulation and increasing nuisance trip risk across process equipment. Brief outage events carry disproportionate operational cost in cold-chain operations. **Simulated MPTS Outcome**
