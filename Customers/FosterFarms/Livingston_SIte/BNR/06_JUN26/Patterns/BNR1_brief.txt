**BNR-1 Energy Field Summary Brief — Foster Farms, Livingston HQ | June 2026** **Field Condition:**
BNR-1 is operating in an untuned state with a peak demand of 1,515.3 kW against a 1,605 kVA supply ceiling. Power factor averages 89.7 percent with a documented floor of 70.0 percent. Reactive burden holds at 692.6 kVAR average — persistent and structural in character. Total estimated annual waste is approximately $449,367, including roughly $274,706 in reactive losses and approximately $153,778 in cooling costs dedicated to managing transformer heat. **Voltage Stability:**
The voltage field runs in a tight band around 476.5V with a documented maximum of 489.2V. Five to six distinct momentary voltage collapse events were identified across June, with a recorded minimum of 0.0V confirming full field interruptions. Estimated total outage accumulation: 10 to 18 minutes. Device-health risk is classified as high given repeated collapse cycles in a cold-chain environment. **Reactive and Harmonic Findings:**
Average current of 1,918.5 amps; peak 2,059.5 amps. iTHD averages 6.3 percent with a peak of 11.4 percent — both exceeding IEEE 519 limits. Harmonic environment is distributed and persistent, consistent with multiple VFD and motor drive systems. Thermal burden documented at 602,911 BTU per hour. **Operational Stress:**
Untuned field is drifting toward higher reactive burden over time. Without correction, heat burden and waste costs will trend upward month over month. **MPTS Simulation Outcome:**
Post-correction simulation projects 99.5 percent power factor held continuously. Reactive compensation absorbs the 692.6 kVAR burden at point of correction, reducing thermal load, cooling demand, and reactive losses substantially. **Recommended Engineering Attention:**
Investigate voltage collapse root cause. Assess harmonic filtering needs. Clarify kVAR sub-metric discrepancy. Conduct transformer insulation condition assessment before summer peak season. Planning-level MPTS investment: approximately $200,000 to about $400,000 installed, subject to engineering scope and site conditions. ---
