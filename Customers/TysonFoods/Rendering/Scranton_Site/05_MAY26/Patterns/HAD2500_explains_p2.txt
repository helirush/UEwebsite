HAD2500 demonstrates the effectiveness of your existing MPTS investment while revealing clear optimization opportunities. Your two H240 and three H490 units have successfully improved power factor to 95.4 percent, moving the transformer into energy recovery mode during certain operational cycles where it actually generates power back to the grid. However, voltage stability concerns emerge during peak production — triangular voltage drops from 478 volts down to 300 volts during high-amperage cycling create equipment stress risk. The reactive power demand peaks at 710.2 kiloVAR with amperage cycling between 600 and 2,100 amps, generating substantial thermal burden despite existing MPTS correction. Engineering attention should focus on voltage stability optimization and potential incremental MPTS expansion. One additional H490 unit could close the remaining 4.5 percentage point power factor gap and eliminate the 29 kilowatts of electromagnetic cooling burden. Annual waste cost of about $97,789 represents the remaining optimization value opportunity. Pricing note: these figures are planning-level estimates, not final quotes. Final installed pricing depends on engineering scope, labor, and site conditions. For planning, estimated installed investment is roughly $83,100 to $112,000.
