MPTS harmonization simulation projects pushing MSB-1's already excellent 98.8% power factor to 99.5%, representing incremental optimization rather than dramatic correction. The projected field maintains identical production output patterns while reducing reactive losses through approximately 30+ kVAR demand reduction. Post-MPTS simulation shows preserved voltage stability and harmonic performance while optimizing the remaining reactive power inefficiencies. For MSB-1, MPTS sizing would require approximately one H490 unit based on the 192.2 kVAR maximum demand, with planning-level installed investment estimated around $200,000. However, the incremental nature of savings from an already high-performing baseline suggests careful economic evaluation of payback periods. The simulation demonstrates that even professionally managed systems can achieve marginal efficiency gains through targeted reactive power compensation.
