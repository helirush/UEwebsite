Electric Service Context

Power Provider for BOA Building: Avista Utilities

BOA Building electricity service in Spokane is delivered by Avista, an investor-owned utility regulated by the Washington Utilities and Transportation Commission (UTC). This utility context supports tariff and cost-behavior interpretation in Unity Energy studies.

Utility Type

Regulatory Model
