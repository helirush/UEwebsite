Unity Audio Pre-Recording Checklist (Required)

1) Edit narration text in:
   ./audio/unity-executive-intro-script-approved.txt

2) Open on-screen review page:
   ./unity-intro-script-review.html
   Confirm wording, pacing, and final line endings.

3) Obtain explicit text approval before generating MP3.

4) Generate MP3 using only the approved narration file.
   Do not include production notes in the narration input.

5) Keep direction notes in:
   ./audio/unity-executive-intro-production-notes.txt
