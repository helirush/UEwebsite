Page 1 · Cover / Purpose

What began as measurement is becoming understanding.

Unity Energy and Foster Farms have built a long-term observation environment that now supports electrical visibility, institutional knowledge, and emerging operational intelligence.

This briefing is designed to acknowledge trust, summarize what has been quietly established, and frame a roadmap for the next phase of collaboration.

Page 2 · What Has Been Established

Long-duration electrical visibility is now in place.

Over multiple years, sustained monitoring across Foster Farms facilities has created historical electrical behavior context that can be reviewed, compared, and interpreted across operating environments.

Foundational Coverage

  • Cherry Avenue and Livingston historical observation context
  • One-second and one-minute streaming infrastructure
  • Transformer and voltage behavior visibility

Operational Continuity

  • Monthly reporting and historical trend interpretation
  • Anomaly-awareness examples, including T15 behavior reviews
  • Persistent reporting infrastructure for executive review

Recognition: Foster Farms created the conditions for long-term learning. We also acknowledge Steve Cash for continuity and support throughout this collaboration.

Historical electrical behavior can now be interpreted in a way that supports faster awareness, improved context for decisions, and selective optimization priorities.
