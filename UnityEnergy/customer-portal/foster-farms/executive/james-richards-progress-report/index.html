Page 1 · Executive Purpose

What began as electrical measurement now supports executive findings and recommended action.

Unity Energy and Foster Farms have completed multiple years of transformer-level measurement and analysis. This brief focuses on what has been learned, why it matters financially, and what action should be evaluated next.

This report is intended to support executive decision-making around:

  • Financial impact and cost-recovery potential
  • Infrastructure planning and capacity utilization
  • Asset performance and operational reliability
  • Demand visibility by facility and utility territory
  • Potential deferral of future capital expenditure
  • Evaluation of a controlled MPTS deployment

The objective is not to restate what was measured; it is to convert measured evidence into a practical next-step recommendation.

Page 4 · Executive Findings & Recommended Action

Executive Findings & Recommended Action

Based on multiple years of measurement and analysis, Unity believes Foster Farms has identifiable infrastructure optimization opportunities that now justify evaluation of a controlled MPTS deployment.

What We Have Learned

  • Multiple years of operational electrical data have now been collected
  • Baselines have been established across monitored facilities
  • Site-level electrical behavior can now be compared with confidence
  • Significant infrastructure optimization opportunities have been identified

What The Data Indicates

  • Capacity recovery opportunities exist
  • Thermal burden reduction opportunities exist
  • Reactive energy management opportunities exist
  • Operational efficiency opportunities exist

Business Implications

  • Potential reduction in electrical stress on existing assets
  • Improved utilization of existing infrastructure capacity
  • Potential avoidance or deferral of future capital expenditure
  • Measurable operational and financial value if validated in-field

Recommended Next Step

  • Initiate a controlled MPTS deployment at the highest-opportunity location
  • Measure results against current transformer baseline behavior
  • Validate operational impact and financial outcomes
  • Complete Livingston field-level environmental calibration in parallel
  • Develop a broader deployment roadmap based on measured results

After multiple years of measurement, analysis, and operational observation, Unity believes Foster Farms now possesses sufficient visibility to move from measurement to targeted infrastructure optimization.

The recommended next step is a controlled deployment and calibration pass designed to validate measurable operational and financial outcomes while preserving conservative commitments.

Additional transformer-level studies, narrated field summaries, and historical reporting archives are presently accessible within the private Foster Farms environment.
