The Visibility Principle is simple.

Humans cannot steward what they cannot understand.

And they cannot understand what they cannot see.

For generations, industrial electricity has carried consequences that were difficult to observe directly: heat, distortion, reactive burden, capacity loss, equipment stress, and wasted energy.

Unity brings those consequences into view.

Visibility reveals behavior.

Behavior reveals consequences.

And once consequences become visible, better decisions become possible.
