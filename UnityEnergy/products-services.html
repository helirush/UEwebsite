Unity Service Model

Industrial Field Operations:
Managed Monitoring, Harmonization and Support

Unity deployments provide continuous field monitoring, harmonization, reporting, and operational support for industrial electrical systems. Organizations may operate independently using Unity systems and training, or partner directly with Unity through managed field operations and ongoing support.

Included with every new installed system: Year One managed service. From commissioning forward, Unity provides monitoring, reporting, and guided operating support. After Year One, customers typically continue in Unity-Managed Basic or Advanced care.

Service Track A — Unity-Managed 24/7 Field Health Path (Primary)

Unity-Managed service is the primary path. Because this work is new to most organizations, Unity remains your operating partner with continuous oversight, interpretation, and response support.

Managed Levels

Basic and Advanced Care

  • Basic: 24/7 field monitoring and health oversight.
  • Basic: Standard reporting cadence across behavior, voltage, and heat indicators.
  • Advanced: Expanded behavior analysis and guided optimization for complex site conditions.
  • Advanced: Optional interactive Clerk voice support for walkthroughs, Q&A, and operator handoffs.
Best Fit

Organizations That Want Managed Outcomes

  • Teams that want minimal internal operating load.
  • Sites that need around-the-clock field confidence and escalation support.
  • Organizations prioritizing continuity and outsourced expertise.

Service Track B — Customer-Led Learning Path (Optional)

Customer-led operation remains available for teams that want to run the system internally. Unity provides structured training, standards, and periodic expert reviews.

What You Get

Training + Operating Enablement

  • Onboarding workshops for engineering and operations teams.
  • How to read field behavior, burden patterns, and harmonization signals.
  • Decision playbooks for normal operation, anomalies, and escalation.
  • Scheduled Unity review sessions to validate consistency and performance.
Best Fit

Organizations Building Internal Energy Intelligence

  • Teams that want direct ownership of field operations.
  • Sites with internal technical staff and time for training.
  • Programs focused on long-term in-house capability development.

Managed Care Levels After Year One

At renewal, the default path is Unity-Managed service with two levels of care. This keeps the model simple while matching support depth to site needs.

Basic — Managed Field Health

Continuous monitoring, routine field-health reporting, and standard alert escalation with Unity oversight.

Advanced — Managed Field Intelligence

Everything in Basic plus deeper behavior analysis (voltage, heat, and field dynamics), guided optimization, and optional interactive Clerk voice support.

  • Unity-Managed is the primary long-term recommendation.
  • Customer-led operation remains available by request for trained teams.
  • Both levels keep the same installed Measure • Manage • Exchange platform.
