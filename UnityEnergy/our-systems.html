Unity Energy Platform Stack

Three Integrated Systems: Measure, Manage (Harmonize), and Exchange

Unity’s operating stack begins with field measurement, advances through harmonization at the load, and extends into on-site exchange. This release fully presents Measure and Manage, with Exchange held in controlled preview.

Go to Measure Go to Manage (Harmonize) Go to Exchange

Current release scope: Measure and Manage are active; Exchange remains intentionally limited in preview.

System 1

Measure

AccuVIM metering hardware in protective field-ready enclosure.

  • Real-time electrical field observation across load-critical circuits.
  • Continuous visibility into voltage, current, reactive burden, and harmonics.
  • Measurement foundation for harmonization and exchange operations.

System 2

Manage (Harmonize)

Integrated field installation showing instrumentation and MPTS harmonization in one deployment.

  • MPTS-driven field stabilization at active industrial loads.
  • Reactive burden reduction and harmonic governance at deployment points.
  • Supports low-impedance field behavior and improved transfer conditions.

Exchange
