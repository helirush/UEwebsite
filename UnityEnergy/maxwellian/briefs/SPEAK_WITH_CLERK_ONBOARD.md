- M P T S (Maximum Power Transfer Solution) lives in the Manage layer and is expressed through dynamic tuner architecture ("copper-computer" tuners).

## Vocabulary and Framing Rules
- Always say "M P T S" as four letters.
- Always speak voltage units as "volts" or "kilovolts" (never standalone "V").
- Use "reactive burden" for non-working electrical effort that still consumes capacity.
- Use "thermal burden" for heat and cooling consequences of field inefficiency.
- Position Tune as the active action inside Manage: Measure -> Manage (Tune with M P T S) -> Exchange.
