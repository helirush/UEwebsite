## 1. One sentence

Unity Energy measures, understands, and tunes **transformer-defined industrial electrical energy fields** so equipment can keep doing useful work while unnecessary electrical burden is reduced — and so customer and utility can verify what changed.

## 2. Operating law

**Measure → Manage → Exchange**

| Stage | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Measure** | Continuous observation of the energy field (eStream / field intelligence): work, current, apparent power, reactive behavior, harmonics, demand, loading, time |
| **Manage** | Interpret the field; where evidence supports it, **Tune** (Base Tune / Demand Tune path) using MPTS-class field control — not one-off gadget folklore |
| **Exchange** | Make the invisible visible: boards, reports, portals, verified narratives customer and (increasingly) utility can act on |

**MPTS** is spoken **M-P-T-S** (four letters). It lives inside **Manage** as the tuning / field-control capability — not as a substitute for measurement or verification.

## 3. What an “energy field” is

A **transformer-defined electrical environment** on the customer side of the meter: the last practical node before electrical energy becomes useful work in motors, drives, refrigeration, data halls, process loads, etc.

Unity does **not** begin by installing hardware.  
Unity **begins by measuring**.

## 4. What Unity is / is not

### Is
- Industrial electrical **field stewardship**
- Continuous measurement + understanding + selective tuning + verification
- Customer-side improvement with **upstream system consequences** worth a utility conversation
- A bridge: meter stays the **responsibility** boundary; it need not stay the **understanding** boundary

### Is not
- Mere “power factor correction product” pitch
- Software-only dashboard theater without field truth
- A promise of universal % savings
- Ownership of the customer’s plant by the utility
- Claim that Unity performed the entire 2001-era device R&D arc

## 6. Technology spine (speak carefully)

- **eStream** — continuous field observation and relationship analysis  
- **Base Tune** — persistent non-working / reactive / distortion-related RMS burden while preserving useful work (evidence-gated)  
- **Demand Tune / storage / tariff-aware paths** — peaks, cycling, coincidence (where deployed)  
- **MPTS** — engineered to become a **preferred low-impedance path** for nonproductive/harmonic current within the field, changing current distribution and reducing burden on neighboring assets  
- **Verification** — keep measuring after change

## 8. Customer value (questions, not guarantees)

- Improve the electrical environment inside the facility?  
- Release transformer capacity?  
- Reduce unnecessary current?  
- Better information on how the field actually behaves?  

Answer with measurement and site evidence — never generic percentages.

## 9. Utility value (conversation, not closed sale)

Same physics; **who captures value** depends on utility organism (co-op, muni, IOU, restructured).

Open questions only until verified:
- Feeder/substation headroom?  
- Losses?  
- Purchased-power / capacity / coincidence?  
- Deferred infrastructure?  
- Who should fund measurement vs tuning capital?

**First step for utilities:** visibility into consequential customer fields — not “buy a tuner.”  
Measurement-sponsored portfolios → selective tune where evidence and economics justify.

## 10. Commercial posture

- Models may include customer-funded, utility-sponsored measurement, shared cost, performance verification — **organism-specific**
